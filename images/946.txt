The skirt is a very short, flippy glossy black satin mini skirt with a high waistband, wrapped diagonally and gathered at one hip into a cascading draped ruffle. It flares out with bouncy volume and ends high on the upper thigh, well above mid-thigh. Footwear consists of black satin pointed-toe stiletto pumps. Accessories include small diamond stud earrings. COLOR PALETTE: black, beige.
