This casual streetwear outfit features a warm monochromatic palette dominated by rich brown tones, layered over contrasting off-white base pieces.
The upper layers consist of an oversized, cropped dark brown puffer jacket in structured nylon with thick horizontal quilted baffles, a high stand-up collar, and a white embroidered logo on the left chest. Underneath is a loose, off-white fleece hoodie with a large slouchy hood and a relaxed hemline. On the lower body are relaxed, straight-leg dark brown denim jeans featuring a high-waisted construction and prominent distressed rips across both knees.
On the feet are chunky, low-top white leather sneakers with a thick flat sole. Wearable accessories include a delicate, layered gold-toned chain necklace resting over the hoodie neckline.
