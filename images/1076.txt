This contemporary utilitarian streetwear and techwear-inspired ensemble features a stark, high-contrast palette dominated by matte black and muted olive-drab green, accented with polished silver metal hardware. The upper layer is a tightly fitted, lightweight matte black knit turtleneck top with a high, snug mock collar and slim, full-length sleeves that tuck seamlessly into the waistband, leaving only the hands bare. Layered tightly over the bust and shoulders is a structured, semi-gloss black leather chest harness constructed from medium-width straps connected by large silver O-rings on the sides, a central front buckle, and vertical shoulder-adjusting buckles. Dominating the lower silhouette are voluminous, relaxed-fit olive-green cargo joggers crafted from crisp, medium-weight woven cotton-nylon twill that holds a structured, slightly rumpled drape. These high-waisted trousers feature deep front pleats, elasticized ankle cuffs, multiple oversized bellows cargo pockets stacked along both thighs and calves with hanging black nylon webbing straps and silver D-rings, and horizontal black buckled leg straps wrapping tightly around the mid-thighs. A black leather waist belt with a matte black buckle secures the waist, featuring a front silver D-ring with a long, dangling black webbing strap. Heavy, chunky black leather lace-up combat boots with thick lugged platform soles, silver eyelets, and wrapped silver curb-chain ankle accents ground the look. Layered silver curb chains with a small rectangular pendant complete the neckline.
