This modern romantic casual outfit features a delicate neutral color palette dominated by creamy ivory-white and warm sandy beige, accented by deep black leather and gold hardware.
The primary top is a completely unlined, sheer fitted blouse crafted entirely from a single layer of matching semi-sheer cream-colored floral lace across both the bodice and the sleeves. It features a crew neckline, subtle puff shoulders, small round gold front buttons, and long sleeves flaring into dramatic, fluid oversized bell cuffs. The sheer blouse is tucked into high-waisted, full-length sand-beige trousers made of a mid-weight wool-blend cloth, tailored into a distinct banana-pants silhouette that is voluminous through the hips with deep front pleats, then slightly tapers down toward the ankles. The waist is defined by a textured black leather belt with a gold rectangular buckle.
Footwear consists of matching sand-beige leather dress shoes with a sharp pointed toe line and a thin stiletto high heel.
