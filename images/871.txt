This contemporary feminine outfit features a color palette dominated by white and black, with a prominent pastel pink accent and no graphic patterns.
The upper garment is a form-fitting camisole top in soft, lustrous pastel pink satin. It features ultra-thin spaghetti straps and a draped cowl neckline that sits low on the chest, leaving the collarbones, shoulders, and arms exposed. The lower garment is a high-waisted white mini-skirt crafted from a thick, structured fabric that holds its shape. It features dense, uniform knife pleats that flare out slightly, ending at the mid-thigh.
A medium-width black leather belt with a rectangular silver buckle cinches the waist. The legs are covered in sheer black tights. On the feet are classic pointed-toe pumps in smooth black leather. Wearable accessories include a layered silver chain necklace with a small hoop pendant, wrist-length black fabric gloves, and multiple thin silver rings and chain bracelets worn over the gloves.
