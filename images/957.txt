This modern evening outfit features a high-contrast color palette dominated by sleek matte and satin black, balanced by a highly voluminous chocolate-brown outer layer and refined gold accents.
The primary silhouette-defining piece is an ultra-voluminous, cropped bubble jacket made of thick, plush brown fur, constructed with extreme oversized proportions and distinct segmented, puffy quilted sections creating a heavy 3D grid texture. It layers over an architectural, skin-tight black satin corset bodysuit featuring a structural sweetheart neckline, defined underwire cups, vertical boning, and a dramatic flared peplum at the hips, leaving the upper chest, shoulders, and arms fully exposed.
The lower body is covered by opaque black stirrup leggings in a thin, highly elastic matte fabric that contours tightly to the legs.
Accessories include high-fashion knee-high black leather boots with a wide, structured fold-over shaft adorned with a gold padlock pendant, alongside a delicate gold chain bracelet and matching stud earrings featuring repeating black quatrefoil motifs.
