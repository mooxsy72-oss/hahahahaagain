OUTFIT: This airy party mini dress is made entirely of layered burnt-orange silk chiffon. It has thin spaghetti straps and a straight neckline wrapped in softly draped, crisscrossing chiffon folds across the bust. The fitted corset-style bodice has vertical seams and boning that define the waist. A draped chiffon sash wraps diagonally across the waist and gathers into a twisted knot at one hip, from which a long cascading ruffle falls past the hem. The short, very full skirt is built from several layered chiffon ruffles with rolled wavy edges, creating light, floaty volume and ending high on the upper thigh. Footwear consists of nude leather strappy stiletto sandals. Accessories include small gold drop earrings. COLOR PALETTE: burnt orange, nude, gold.
