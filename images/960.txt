This high-contrast, sporty winter outfit features a striking color distribution dominated by a seamless, uniform solid white base, contrasted by a voluminous dark burgundy-red outer layer and subtle silver hardware accents.
The most visually prominent piece is a cropped, extremely oversized puffer jacket made of a glossy, high-shine burgundy fabric, constructed with thick, heavily padded horizontal quilted sections, a massive plush spread collar, and voluminous rounded sleeves. Layered underneath is a skin-tight, full-coverage white spandex catsuit jumpsuit featuring a tall mock neck, long slim sleeves, and a vertical front zipper.
Matching the jumpsuit perfectly in color and material, the footwear consists of structured, knee-high white leather boots featuring a clean pointed toe line and a flat sole profile, creating an uninterrupted white line from torso to toe.
