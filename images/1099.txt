This high-fashion outfit features a monochrome, tailored business-chic look with an intentionally elongated, ultra-cinched silhouette. The dominant garment is a structured, matte black tunic-length blazer jacket with sharp, squared padded shoulders, tight short sleeves finished with thin white contrast cuffs, and a deep V-neckline secured by four round silver buttons. Layered underneath is a crisp, white, fine-textured collared shirt featuring a subtle vertical pinstripe texture, buttoned tightly up to a pointed collar. The lower body is covered by secondary sheer black vertically ribbed tights that fully encase the extremely elongated legs. Completing the ensemble are glossy, high-shine black patent leather stiletto pumps with sharply pointed toes and very high, thin heels.
