This academic-inspired preppy outfit features a strict monochrome color distribution dominated by solid black, balanced by a secondary crisp white layer and small metallic gold and silver details.
The base layer is an oversized, thigh-length white button-up shirtdress made of smooth, lightweight cotton poplin. It features a sharp pointed collar and long bishop sleeves that gather loosely into structured buttoned cuffs. Over it sits a loose-fitting black V-neck vest dress crafted from thick, structured wool felt, featuring a decorative row of three black buttons on the left shoulder. The vest dress is shorter than the white shirt, allowing the straight white hem to extend past it.
The silhouette is cinched at the natural waist by a narrow black leather belt with a square silver buckle and a delicate silver chain detail draping onto the right hip. The legs are covered in opaque solid black tights. On the feet are knee-high black suede boots featuring structured block heels. Accessories include thick gold hoop earrings and a delicate beaded bracelet with alternating black and gold beads.
