This elegant minimal smart-casual outfit features a clean neutral color distribution dominated by solid jet-black and a warm sand-beige tone, accented by silver hardware and leather textures.
The upper garment is a relaxed-fit, short-sleeved knit polo shirt in solid sand-beige, crafted from a lightweight, fine-gauge cotton knit. It features an open, unbuttoned V-neckline with a flat collar and straight cuffs, providing full torso coverage. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, tailored trousers in solid black, made from a structured wool suiting fabric. They feature a loose, relaxed straight-leg silhouette with vertical pleats and cuffed hems.
A medium-width black leather belt with a rectangular silver buckle cinches the waist. On the feet are chunky black patent-leather penny loafers with thick, lugged soles. The sole wearable accessory is a classic wristwatch featuring a rectangular gold case, white dial, and a black alligator-leather strap.
