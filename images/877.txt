This safari-inspired contemporary summer outfit features a warm neutral color distribution dominated by a solid off-white tone, balanced by a secondary taupe-beige and dark brown leather accents.
The upper garment is a tight, form-fitting tank top in solid taupe-beige, crafted from a lightweight knit fabric with a distinct, fine vertical ribbed texture. It features a deep scoop neckline and sleeveless construction, fully exposing the arms, collarbones, and upper chest while being neatly tucked into the waistband. The lower garment consists of high-waisted tailored shorts in a structured, mid-weight off-white cotton twill that retains its shape. They feature a fitted waistband, front vertical pleats that create subtle volume, side pockets, and thick cuffed hems ending at the upper thigh.
A dark brown leather belt with a small, rounded brass buckle cinches the waist. On the feet are matching dark brown leather slide sandals featuring a wide, crisscross woven strap design and flat soles.
