This cottagecore-inspired summer outfit features a fresh color palette dominated by a solid white base and matching yellow footwear.
The primary ensemble is a coordinating two-piece white eyelet cotton set. The top is a form-fitting cropped camisole with a straight neckline and thin shoulder straps that tie into dainty bows, completely exposing the shoulders, collarbones, and a sliver of the midriff. It pairs with a matching high-waisted, structured A-line mini skirt featuring a scalloped hemline that exposes the thighs. Both garments are densely covered with an all-over embroidered pattern of white daisies with bright yellow centers.
On the feet are yellow leather strappy sandals with flat soles and thin laces tied around the ankles.
