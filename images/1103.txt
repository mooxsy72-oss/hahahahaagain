This modern autumn outfit features a balanced color palette of tan-brown, crisp white, and solid black. The central volume comes from a crisp white shirt featuring dramatically oversized, puffed bishop sleeves that gather loosely at wide, structured cuffs. Layered over it is a thick, cropped tan-brown knit vest with a prominent ribbed turtleneck collar and a matching ribbed hemline sitting directly on top of the skirt's waistband.
Below the vest, a high-waisted, completely solid black mini skirt crafted from heavyweight woven fabric extends uninterrupted from the waist to mid-thigh, featuring a sharp side slit that partially exposes the thigh. Footwear consists of chunky black leather Chelsea boots with thick, deeply cleated platform soles and elastic side panels. Accessories include a textured tan teddy-fleece bucket hat, a structured black leather tote bag with top handles, thick gold hoop earrings, and a chunky gold ring.
