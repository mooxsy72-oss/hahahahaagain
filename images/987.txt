This skate-inspired streetwear outfit features a relaxed, oversized silhouette with a balanced color distribution of washed charcoal grey, light blue denim, and crisp black and white accents. The top is a heavy, structured charcoal grey cotton crewneck t-shirt with exaggerated dropped shoulders, wide sleeves reaching the elbows, and an elongated hem. The chest features a faded white collegiate-style graphic reading "MONTPELLIER RUGBY CLUB". This is loosely tucked into voluminous, wide-leg light blue denim shorts that feature a classic five-pocket construction, a high waist, and a length that extends past the knees. Forearms, neck, and lower knees are exposed. The lower legs are covered by high-calf white crew socks with two black horizontal stripes. On the feet are classic black low-top canvas skate shoes with a white side stripe and white rubber waffle soles. A slim silver chain necklace with a small pendant, a black leather belt with a dangling key ring, and a black digital watch complete the look.
