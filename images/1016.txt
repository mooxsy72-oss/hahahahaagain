The monochromatic, layered winter streetwear outfit utilizes varying shades of matte gray and charcoal, focusing entirely on a heavily volume-driven, oversized silhouette with solid textures. The main source of volume is an intentionally cropped, exaggeratedly bulky puffer jacket in heather gray, constructed with thick horizontal quilting, exceptionally wide sleeves, and a massive, structural stand-up collar. Layered underneath are a medium-gray half-zip hoodie with the hood pulled up, a dark gray textured sweater peeking out at the collar, and a longer light gray t-shirt extending past the waist. The bottom half consists of relaxed-fit, full-coverage charcoal gray fleece sweatpants that drape loosely down the legs before slightly bunching around the ankles. The footwear includes dark gray low-top sneakers with a contrasting cream-colored rubber toe cap and thick cream laces, paired with a ribbed gray knit beanie pulled low over the forehead.
