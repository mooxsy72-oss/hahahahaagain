This ensemble belongs to a survival horror and tactical civilian-operator tradition, characterized by practical layering and field-ready gear. Slate blue, charcoal black, and muted gray dominate the palette, accented by weathered leather and metallic hardware. The primary upper layer consists of a fitted, dusty slate-blue ribbed henley tank top worn over a light gray undershirt. Fitted dark charcoal-black denim jeans form the base, paired with tall black lace-up combat boots. Tactical gear heavily outfits the torso and legs: a black leather shoulder holster harness with a mounted flashlight, a heavy black utility belt featuring a metallic police badge and hip pouches, and a drop-leg holster strapped to the right thigh. Fingerless black tactical gloves, a black fabric forearm band on the left arm, and a simple cord necklace complete the look.
