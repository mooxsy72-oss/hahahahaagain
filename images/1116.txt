This casual winter streetwear outfit features a soft, pastel color palette dominated by off-white and cream tones, with a dusty pink jacket providing a single contrast color.
The core layers consist of a chunky, cream-white ribbed knit turtleneck sweater with an elongated hem covering the hips. Over it is an intentionally bulky, cropped dusty-pink puffer jacket featuring thick horizontal quilted baffles, a high insulated stand-up collar, and wide sleeves. On the lower body are loose, oversized cream-colored sweatpants made of thick jersey, featuring a relaxed silhouette, drawstring waist, and softly elasticated cuffs.
On the feet are plush, cream-white winter snow boots with thick flat soles and front cross-lacing. A matching white ribbed knit beanie completes the coordinated winter look.
