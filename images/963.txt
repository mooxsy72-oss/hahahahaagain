This tailored, menswear-inspired casual outfit features a solid neutral palette dominated by deep charcoal grey, jet black, and crisp white, grounded by classic black footwear.
The primary layered top consists of a relaxed-fit button-up shirt in crisp white cotton poplin, styled with a classic pointed collar and long sleeves rolled up to the mid-forearm. It is layered beneath a fitted, tailored vest waistcoat in structured black suiting fabric, featuring a sharp V-neckline, five front buttons, welt pockets, and a pointed front hem. The shirt is tucked into high-waisted, wide-leg trousers crafted from thick, structure-retaining charcoal-grey wool twill with pronounced front pleats, cinched at the waist by a thin brown leather belt with a small gold buckle.
Completing the look are sleek black leather dress shoes featuring a pointed toe line and a high stiletto heel.
