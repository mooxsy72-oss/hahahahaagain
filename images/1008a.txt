The outfit presents a minimalist, smart-casual summer aesthetic composed of a tank top, pleated trousers, a belt, sneakers, and a wristwatch. The dominant colors are taupe and solid white, accented by deep brown leather details.

Accessories include a metallic silver watch with a circular face and low-top white leather sneakers with matching white laces.
