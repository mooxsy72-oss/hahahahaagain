OUTFIT: This cozy autumn menswear look pairs a classic fisherman knit with textured tailored trousers. The dominant garment is a chunky oatmeal-cream Aran sweater in thick wool with a ribbed crew neck, raised cable and diamond panels down the front, set-in sleeves, and a regular fit, loosely tucked at the waist. It pairs with high-waisted dark brown wide-wale corduroy trousers with double front pleats in a regular straight fit, neither slim nor wide. A dark brown leather belt with a brass buckle defines the waist. Footwear consists of dark brown suede chukka boots. COLOR PALETTE: oatmeal cream, dark brown, brass.
