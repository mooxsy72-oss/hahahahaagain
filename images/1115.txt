This casual streetwear outfit features a vibrant color palette dominated by a mustard-yellow top and washed light-blue denim, balanced by solid white footwear.
The primary garment is an oversized, slouchy mustard-yellow knit sweater in a medium-weight ribbed texture. It features a simple crew neckline, dropped shoulders, and loose sleeves gathered into ribbed cuffs. The chest is decorated with large, blocky white text reading "LOVE". Below are relaxed-fit, high-waisted light-blue denim boyfriend jeans with heavy distressing, featuring prominent ripped cutouts across both knees and thighs. The lower hems are casually cuffed, exposing the ankles.
On the feet are chunky low-top white athletic sneakers with layered panels and thick, textured platform soles. Symmetrical long silver dangle earrings add a delicate metallic accent.
