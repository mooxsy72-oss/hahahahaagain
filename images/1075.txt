This ensemble belongs to a survival horror and rugged casual streetwear tradition, defined by practical, layered textures. Distressed reddish-brown leather, charcoal gray, and washed black dominate the palette, accented by muted metal hardware. The outer layer is a weathered brown leather biker jacket with side waist buckles and zippered pockets, worn open over a dark charcoal zip-up hoodie and a soft light-gray scoop-neck t-shirt. Fitted, heavily distressed washed-black denim jeans complete the lower half.
