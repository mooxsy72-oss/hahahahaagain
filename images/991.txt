This minimalist streetwear outfit features a stark color-blocked distribution dominated by a solid black top and secondary light beige trousers. The top is a heavy, structured cotton crewneck t-shirt with an oversized silhouette, characterized by distinct dropped shoulders and wide half-sleeves extending to the elbows. It hangs loosely past the waist, overlapping a pair of voluminous, wide-leg beige trousers. Made from a mid-weight woven material with soft drape, the trousers feature vertical ironed pleats down the center leg and pool slightly over the footwear. Forearms and neck are the only exposed skin. On the feet are structured, low-top all-white leather sneakers with thick white rubber cup soles. A classic black watch with a leather strap wraps the left wrist.
