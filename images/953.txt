OUTFIT: This casual modern street look pairs a warm tan suede jacket with sleek black basics. The dominant garment is an oversized cropped bomber jacket in soft camel-tan suede with a ribbed stand collar, a gold metal front zip, dropped shoulders, and voluminous balloon sleeves gathered into ribbed cuffs, ending at the waist with a ribbed hem, worn open. Beneath it is a fitted black short-sleeve crewneck t-shirt in smooth stretch jersey. It pairs with high-waisted black leggings with a wide flat waistband. Footwear consists of chestnut suede ultra-short sheepskin boots with a light rubber sole. Accessories include chunky gold hoop earrings. COLOR PALETTE: camel tan, black, chestnut, gold.
