OUTFIT: This modern autumn street look centers on a sleek black knit dress with cozy contrasting footwear. The dominant garment is a black ribbed knit sleeveless mini dress with a high mock neck, a fine vertical rib texture, and a close body-hugging fit ending at the upper thigh, leaving the arms and shoulders uncovered. Footwear consists of olive suede ultra-short sheepskin boots on a chunky platform sole, worn with slouchy white knit socks bunched above them. Accessories include chunky gold teardrop earrings and thin gold bangles. COLOR PALETTE: black, olive khaki, white, gold.
