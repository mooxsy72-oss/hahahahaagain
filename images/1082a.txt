This contemporary streetwear ensemble contrasts a cropped sporty top with dramatically oversized denim, featuring a palette of burgundy, crisp white, and muted olive-gray denim. A medium-weight cotton jersey rugby polo shirt is cropped abruptly beneath the bust, leaving the entire midriff exposed. It features a crisp white polo collar, open placket, and wide horizontal burgundy and white stripes across the loose bodice and slouchy, full-length sleeves with white ribbed cuffs. Dominating the lower silhouette are low-rise, baggy wide-leg jeans in heavy, washed olive-gray denim with classic five-pocket construction, pooling over pointed-toe burgundy heels. A long gold lariat necklace completes the look.
