This contemporary streetwear ensemble is monochromatic matte black with crisp white sneaker accents, contrasting minimal upper coverage with voluminous pants. A tightly fitted, sleeveless black stretch-spandex monokini features a single left shoulder strap, an oval cutout at the center chest, and only a narrow strip of black fabric running down the right side to connect the bust to ultra-high-cut bikini bottoms pulled high above the hip bones, leaving the right shoulder, arms, entire bare midriff, stomach, and left torso completely exposed. Dominating the lower silhouette are low-rise, baggy black cargo joggers in heavy woven fabric with elasticized waistbands, exaggeratedly long dangling black waist straps, stacked knee ruching, hanging ankle straps, and black-and-white high-top basketball sneakers.
