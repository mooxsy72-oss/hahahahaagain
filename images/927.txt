OUTFIT: This sharp autumn menswear look contrasts a dark heavy knit with light warm trousers. The dominant garment is a chunky black cable-knit turtleneck sweater in thick wool with a high folded collar, raglan sleeves, twisted cable panels down the front and sleeves, and a regular fit, loosely tucked at the waist. It pairs with high-waisted sand-beige wool trousers with double front pleats and a clean crease, in a regular straight fit, neither slim nor wide. A dark brown leather belt with a brass buckle defines the waist. Footwear consists of dark brown leather penny loafers. COLOR PALETTE: black, sand beige, dark brown, brass.
