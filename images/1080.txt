This contemporary bridal-inspired mini ensemble is monochromatic pure white, dominated by a structured satin mini dress paired with sheer opera gloves and heels. The sleeveless dress features a fitted bustier bodice with underwire cups, princess seams, and thin spaghetti straps, leaving the shoulders, collarbone, upper chest, and legs exposed. The bodice cinches at the waist before flaring slightly into a crisp, structured A-line skirt ending mid-thigh. Medium-weight matte-sheen satin holds its smooth shape, decorated with dense three-dimensional white floral lace appliqués cascading diagonally from the bust across the lower skirt and straps. Reaching past the elbows, fitted sheer white tulle gloves feature matching scattered 3D flower appliqués along the arms. Sleek white pointed-toe heels complete the outfit.
