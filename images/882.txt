This contemporary loungewear-inspired athletic outfit features a balanced color distribution dominated by a solid light-beige tone, paired with a secondary solid jet-black and minimalist silver accents.
The upper garment is a tight, form-fitting cropped sports bra top in solid black, made from a smooth, elastic performance knit fabric. It features a scoop neckline and thick shoulder straps, leaving the arms, shoulders, collarbones, and a wide section of the midriff fully exposed. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, floor-length wide-leg lounge trousers crafted from fluid, lightweight off-white silk or satin with a subtle sheen. They feature a thick, gathered elastic waistband adjusted by a matching fabric drawstring bow and functional side pockets.
On the feet are flat black leather slide sandals featuring thin, crisscross straps. A delicate, thin silver chain necklace finishes the look.
