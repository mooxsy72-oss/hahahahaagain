This casual summer streetwear outfit relies on a clean, athletic color distribution dominated by solid white and light heather grey, accented by deep burgundy. The top layer is a crisp white cotton crewneck t-shirt with a classic, semi-fitted silhouette and short sleeves that are tightly rolled up at the cuffs. Below, very short, tight-fitting athletic sweat shorts in a light grey jersey fabric hit high on the mid-thigh, featuring an elasticized drawstring waistband.
Mid-calf white ribbed sports socks lead into bulky, retro-style light grey athletic sneakers with layered wave paneling and thick translucent rubber soles.
Accessories add strong visual contrast: a thin gold-toned Cuban link chain accents the neck, a chunky black digital sports watch sits on the left wrist, and a gold metal link bracelet wraps the right wrist.
