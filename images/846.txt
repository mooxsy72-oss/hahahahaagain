A modern Y2K-inspired streetwear look, dominated by black with a single white accent. Oversized black faux-leather bomber jacket, unzipped, with a ribbed stand collar, dropped shoulders, voluminous bloused sleeves gathering into ribbed cuffs, ribbed hem, and a glossy, soft, slightly creased drape; hip-length, it dominates the upper silhouette. Beneath it, a fitted white ribbed-knit cropped sleeveless top with a high crew neckline, ending above the waist and exposing the midriff. Black faux-leather micro pleated mini skirt with a high, structured waistband and stiff box pleats flaring outward. Sheer black tights cover the legs. Chunky white ribbed cuffs peek above black knee-high platform boots with thick lug soles and glossy smooth shafts.
