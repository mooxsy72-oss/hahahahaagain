This contemporary streetwear ensemble contrasts a minimalist cropped top with dramatically oversized denim in a palette of faded sage-olive and matte black. A tight, sleeveless black ribbed-knit crop top with a scoop neckline ends abruptly beneath the chest, leaving the shoulders, arms, upper chest, and entire midriff bare. Dominating the lower silhouette are low-rise, baggy wide-leg jeans in heavy, washed sage-green denim with diagonal front seams, pooling at the floor over beige square-toe sandals. A black long-sleeve sweatshirt featuring white Gothic lettering printed along the sleeves ties loosely around the hips as a layered waist accent.
