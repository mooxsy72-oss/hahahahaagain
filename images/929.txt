OUTFIT: This minimalist autumn menswear look relies on a textured grey knit and dark tailoring. The dominant garment is a chunky heather-grey honeycomb-knit sweater in thick wool with a high mock neck, a deep three-dimensional waffle texture, dropped shoulders, and a regular fit, loosely tucked at the waist. It pairs with high-waisted black wool trousers with double front pleats and a sharp crease, in a regular straight fit, neither slim nor wide. A black leather belt with a matte silver buckle defines the waist. Footwear consists of black leather loafers. COLOR PALETTE: heather grey, black, silver.
