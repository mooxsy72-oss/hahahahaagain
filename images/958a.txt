This modern winter outfit features a monochromatic color palette dominated by sleek solid black and silver hardware details.
The primary silhouette-defining piece is a fitted, tight-cut black top with structured, padded power shoulders, a high mock-neckline, and long, slim sleeves providing complete upper-body coverage.
The lower body is covered by high-waisted, slim-fit black leggings made of elastic, slightly shiny stretch-fabric, featuring subtle front split hems at the ankles.
Wearable accessories include knee-high flat black leather boots with a structured shaft decorated with a embossed tonal logo motif, a thin metallic silver chain belt at the waist, and small silver double-C logo stud earrings.
