This contemporary streetwear ensemble features a vintage-washed palette dominated by faded charcoal black and acid-wash light blue denim, accented with crisp white and metallic silver. Dominating the upper silhouette is an oversized, boxy hip-length t-shirt in heavyweight, washed charcoal-black cotton jersey with a thick ribbed crew collar, dramatically dropped shoulders, and wide elbow-length sleeves, leaving the neck and forearms exposed. Across the chest sits a large, monochrome sepia-gray graphic featuring arched "REPRESENT" lettering above classical winged angel figures and "FORTITUDINE VINCIMUS" text below. Loose, straight-leg jeans in heavy, structured acid-wash light blue denim drape cleanly over crisp white low-top leather court sneakers. Silver-toned accessories include a thin cross pendant necklace, link bracelet, metal wristwatch, and draped pocket chain.
