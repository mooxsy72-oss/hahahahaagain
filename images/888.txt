This vibrant summer clubwear outfit features a monochromatic color distribution dominated entirely by uniform lime-green tones, with no visual patterns.
The primary ensemble is a coordinating two-piece lime-green ribbed knit set. The top is a form-fitting, cropped long-sleeved cardigan with a deep V-neckline. It features a dramatic lace-up front construction secured by thin lime-green cords through fabric loops, completely exposing the chest and a wide section of the midriff. It pairs with a matching high-waisted lime-green mini skirt in a tight, structured bodycon silhouette that exposes the thighs.
On the feet are lime-green leather strappy sandals with flat soles and thin ankle straps. Wearable accessories include chunky lime-green hoop earrings and matching thin bangles on both wrists.
