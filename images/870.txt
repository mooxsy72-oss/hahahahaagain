This fantasy-infused haute couture evening gown features a dominant color palette of rich royal purple and deep violet, accented by a glittering field of silver and translucent crystalline details.
The primary garment is an asymmetric, floor-length gown featuring a fitted sweetheart bodice that heavily pinches the natural waist. The structured bodice is adorned with diagonal draped bands of shimmering purple satin and a dense encrustation of purple and silver sequins along the top edge, leaving the shoulders and collarbones exposed.
The lower half combines two distinct layers to create dramatic visual volume. The base layer is a column-style underskirt made of semi-sheer black tulle, densely dotted with shimmering silver rhinestones and sequins that create a starry effect, exposing the silhouette of the legs. Wrapped over this is an oversized, heavy purple velvet overskirt that is gathered at the right hip, split wide open at the front, and cascades down the sides into a dramatic, sweeping train. The look is accessorized with matching separate opera-length sleeves made of sheer purple mesh, heavily embroidered with glittering sequins.
