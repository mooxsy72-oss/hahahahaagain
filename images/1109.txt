This glamour-infused contemporary outfit features a balanced color distribution dominated by textured light pink and crisp white, highlighted by sparkling and metallic accents.
The upper garment is a heavily structured underbust-to-overbust corset bustier crafted from sheer white mesh and intricate, semi-translucent floral lace panels. It features molded underwire cups with a sweetheart neckline, a severely tapered waist, and a vertical front busk closure with small, polished gold-toned metal hooks.
The lower garment consists of high-waisted, straight-leg pants made from thick, structured denim in a light, slightly washed pink hue. The fabric is completely covered in a dense, all-over field of tiny, glittering silver rhinestones that catch the light. They feature classic five-pocket construction, a zippered fly with a silver metal button, and straight hems. On the feet are sleek, crisp white pointed-toe pumps with high stiletto heels.
