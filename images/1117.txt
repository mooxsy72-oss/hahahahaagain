This casual winter streetwear outfit features a defined contrast between a bulky olive-green upper body and a sleek, form-fitting black lower silhouette, with neutral white accents.
The upper body is dominated by an oversized, cropped olive-green puffer jacket made from structured, quilted nylon with thick horizontal baffles. It features an insulated high stand-up collar, wide long sleeves with elasticated cuffs, and adjustable drawstring toggles at the hem. The lower body is defined by opaque, high-waisted black leggings that tightly hug the waist, hips, and legs down to the lower calves.
On the feet are chunky olive-green chelsea-style boots with thick, lugged rubber soles, worn over slightly scrunched off-white ribbed crew socks. A solid black ribbed knit beanie neatly crowns the look.
