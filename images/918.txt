The outfit is a sophisticated winter ensemble dominated by rich chocolate brown, with mottled grey as a secondary tone. The silhouette is visually anchored by a long, heavy wool overcoat in a solid chocolate brown. It features an oversized, relaxed drape, wide notched lapels, structural shoulders, and straight long sleeves. Underneath is a dense, medium-weight heather grey mock-neck knit sweater tucked tightly into the pants. The high-waisted, straight-leg trousers are made from a thick, heathered grey wool flannel with prominent front pleats and cuffs at the ankle. A slim, dark brown leather belt with a simple metallic buckle secures the high waist. The look is accessorized with dark brown leather gloves and polished dark brown leather oxford dress shoes.
