This monochrome knit outfit features a unified color distribution rendered entirely in a solid light oatmeal-beige tone, grounded by tonal neutral footwear.
The primary set is crafted from a soft, mid-weight brushed knit fabric with high stretch and smooth shape retention. The upper piece is a cropped, blouson-style cardigan top constructed with a deep collarless V-neckline, four matching front buttons, a wide ribbed elastic waistband, and long sleeves with heavy volume slouching loosely into elongated, tight ribbed cuffs. This layers over a matching high-waisted maxi skirt that contours seamlessly through the hips and drapes straight down into a relaxed column silhouette.
Footwear consists of matching oatmeal-beige suede ankle boots featuring a clean pointed toe line and a low, comfortable kitten heel.
