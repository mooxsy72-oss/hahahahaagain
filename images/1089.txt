This contemporary casual streetwear ensemble features a clean color-blocked palette of charcoal gray, light ice-blue washed denim, and crisp white, accented by metallic silver. A relaxed-fit, hip-length crewneck t-shirt in medium-weight matte charcoal-gray cotton jersey features a ribbed crew collar, dropped shoulders, and wide, elbow-length short sleeves, leaving the neck and forearms bare while falling untucked over the waistband. Dominating the lower silhouette are baggy, wide-leg barrel-cut jeans crafted from heavy, structured light-wash ice-blue denim with subtle thigh fading, classic five-pocket construction, and long, loose hems pooling heavily over the footwear. Crisp white low-top leather court sneakers with flat rubber soles and a silver-toned metal link wristwatch with a dark dial complete the look.
