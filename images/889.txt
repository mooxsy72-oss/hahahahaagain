This casual summer streetwear outfit features a playful color palette blending vibrant lime-green accents with solid white layers, anchored by a prominent graphic novelty pattern.
The inner layer consists of a form-fitting lime-green crop top in a fine ribbed knit texture, featuring a deep scoop neckline and sleeveless construction, exposing the collarbones and a sliver of the midriff. Over this is an oversized, fluidly draped white cotton poplin button-down shirt worn completely open with the long sleeves loosely rolled up to the forearms. The lower body is defined by a high-waisted white denim mini skirt featuring a structured, straight A-line silhouette decorated with a large-scale, scattered graphic print of sliced green kiwi fruits.
On the feet are minimalist off-white leather heeled mules with crisscross toe straps and slim kitten heels.
