This monochrome lounge outfit features a unified color distribution rendered entirely in a solid pastel bubblegum-pink tone.
The inner layer is a floor-length, form-fitting maxi dress crafted from a mid-weight ribbed knit fabric with excellent vertical stretch and shape retention. It features a deep V-neckline, thin camisole shoulder straps, a fitted waist profile, and a slightly flared mermaid hemline, leaving the upper chest, collarbones, and arms exposed. Layered over the dress is a matching ankle-length, open-front duster cardigan made of identical pink ribbed knit fabric, constructed with a collarless edge, relaxed dropped shoulders, and loose full-length sleeves.
Footwear consists of matching pink leather heeled sandals featuring a minimalist open-toe strap layout, a delicate ankle wrap closure, and a thin stiletto high heel.
