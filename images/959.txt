This cohesive, monochrome autumn outfit is rendered entirely in a solid dark chocolate-brown palette, accented by warm gold hardware.
The dominant outerwear piece is a voluminous, padded puffer jacket featuring a massive, exaggerated wrap shawl collar, wide long sleeves, and a cinched waist secured by a thin fabric drawstring tie. Underneath layers a secondary fitted, high-coverage jersey top with structured, padded power shoulders, a clean mock neckline, and tight full-length sleeves. This tucks into matching high-waisted tailored shorts made of a thick, structure-retaining wool-blend fabric with crisp front pleats, cinched by a thin matching leather belt with a sleek gold rectangular buckle.
Footwear consists of pointed-toe ankle boots in smooth leather, featuring a very high, thin stiletto heel. Accessories are completed by chunky gold teardrop earrings and a gold watch with a circular dial.
