This ensemble belongs to a sleek tactical espionage and survival horror tradition, balancing form-fitting knitwear with functional leather gear. Deep burgundy and matte black dominate the palette, accented by muted metal hardware. The primary layer is a fitted, ribbed burgundy turtleneck mini dress with long sleeves. Tactical accessories layer directly over the knit: a black leather chest harness with silver buckles, an angled hip belt supporting a black leather drop-leg holster on the left thigh, and short black leather gloves. Sleek, thigh-high black leather boots with high heels and platform soles complete the footwear.
