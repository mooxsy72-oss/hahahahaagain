This sporty, avant-garde streetwear outfit features a black and crisp white color palette defined by deconstructed athletic garments. The upper piece is an ultra-cropped, form-fitting black track jacket in smooth tricot fabric, featuring an asymmetric design with sliced, overlapping panels, a high funnel collar, a partial zipper, and mismatched white three-stripe graphics across the chest and sleeves, leaving the midriff exposed. The lower layer consists of oversized, floor-length black track pants featuring a gathered drawstring waist, a baggy wide-leg silhouette, and complex patchworked construction with slanted white multi-stripe panels, snap-button plackets, and zippered pockets. Grounding the oversized silhouette are chunky, matte black sneakers with an extremely thick, layered triple-sole design and complex paneled construction.
