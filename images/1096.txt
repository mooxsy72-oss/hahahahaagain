This sophisticated, classic-chic ensemble features a dominant black-and-white boucle tweed texture paired with a secondary solid crisp white tone, presenting a structured yet textured aesthetic.
The outermost layer is a tailored, thigh-length tweed blazer jacket crafted from heavy, thick woolen boucle fabric with a dense, small-scale black, grey, and white plaid weave. It features sharp, padded shoulders, notched lapels, and a wide matching tweed belt tied into a soft bow that aggressively cinches the natural waist. The hemline of the jacket is finished with a frayed fringe trim. Beneath it sits a classic white collared dress shirt made of crisp, structured cotton poplin, with its sharp collar popped over the blazer lapels and long cuffs extending past the jacket sleeves.
The lower garment consists of matching high-waisted tweed shorts hidden slightly beneath the blazer hem, also finished with frayed fringe edges. The legs are covered in sheer black tights. On the feet are minimalist white patent-leather stiletto pumps featuring thin ankle straps with small gold buckles.
