Modern winter minimalist look, monochrome in black and charcoal grey, with light grey as the only accent. A black faux-leather cropped biker-style jacket, oversized and boxy, with a tall stand collar fastened by snap tabs, an asymmetric double-breasted front closed by snaps, dropped shoulders with shoulder epaulettes and long full sleeves gathering into buttoned cuffs. The soft, matte, slightly grained fabric has a medium weight, drapes with loose creases and keeps a structured shape. Beneath, a charcoal-grey chunky knit sweater, oversized and cropped at the hip, with a wide ribbed turtleneck, dropped shoulders, full balloon sleeves, and thick ribbed cuffs and hem; thick, soft and warm. Black high-waisted leggings in smooth, matte, stretchy fabric, full-length and body-hugging. Light-grey ribbed legwarmer cuffs, slouchy and bunched, rest at the top of chunky black leather knee-high boots with a buckled strap and a thick lugged sole.
