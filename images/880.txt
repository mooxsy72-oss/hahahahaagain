This elegant resort-style summer outfit features a monochrome palette dominated by solid off-white, balanced by warm brown leather and minimalist gold accents.
The upper garment is a cropped halter-neck top crafted from smooth, lightweight off-white silk with a gentle luster. It features a deep plunging V-neckline, wide supportive straps, and an overlapping wrap-around construction that cinches beneath the bust, leaving the back, shoulders, arms, and midriff fully exposed. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, floor-length trousers made from a mid-weight, structured linen-cotton blend. They feature sharp vertical front pleats that create volume, a straight-leg silhouette, and side pockets.
A thin, dark brown leather belt with a small brass buckle accentuates the waist. On the feet are flat brown leather slide sandals. Wearable accessories include a delicate gold pendant necklace and small pearl drop earrings.
