This chic contemporary outfit features a black and deep burgundy color palette with a structured, layered silhouette. The dominant outer layer is a mid-thigh length, oversized blazer jacket in semi-glossy burgundy faux leather, featuring wide notched lapels, long straight sleeves, and flap pockets. Beneath it is a form-fitting, cropped black knit top with a high turtleneck and short sleeves, leaving the midriff subtly exposed. The bottom layer is a high-waisted, structured black A-line mini skirt that features a curved overlay slit on the left thigh, completely trimmed with a delicate border of white pearls. Tall, tight-fitting black leather over-the-knee boots with high stiletto heels elongate the legs. Small, chunky gold hoop earrings with a textured surface provide a final metallic accent.
