OUTFIT: This cozy modern autumn look pairs sleek black basics with soft cream textures. The dominant garment is a fitted black long-sleeve top in soft stretch jersey with a wide scoop neckline and a close, smooth fit ending at the hips. It pairs with high-waisted black leggings with a wide flat waistband. Chunky cream cable-knit leg warmers are slouched around the lower legs above taupe suede ultra-short sheepskin boots on a thick platform sole. Accessories include small gold hoop earrings. COLOR PALETTE: black, cream, taupe, gold.
