This athleisure ensemble relies on a high-contrast monochrome palette dominated by black, with stark white secondary pieces. The foundation consists of a tight, skin-fitting black elastic crop top with a straight bandeau neckline and thin shoulder straps, paired with matching high-waisted, full-length black leggings that hug the lower body entirely. This tight-fitting base exposes the midriff and arms.
Bright white, vertically ribbed crew socks are pulled taut over the lower leggings. Footwear features chunky white athletic sneakers with technical black and grey structural overlays on the sides.
Accessories include a slim white electronic wristwatch and a thin gold bangle.
