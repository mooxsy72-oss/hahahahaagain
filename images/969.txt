This modern, monochrome knit lounge outfit features a uniform color distribution rendered entirely in a solid pastel lemon-yellow tone, grounded by clean matching footwear.
The primary set is crafted from a lightweight, flexible ribbed knit fabric with prominent vertical lines and a soft, fluid drape. The upper piece is a slim-fit, long-sleeved cardigan top constructed with a pointed polo-style collar and a vertical row of small white buttons, styled partially unbuttoned at the lower hem to expose a small sliver of the midriff. This layers over a matching high-waisted, floor-length maxi skirt that contours through the hips and flows into a column silhouette.
Footwear consists of matching light-beige leather mules featuring a squared open-toe line and a low, chunky block heel.
