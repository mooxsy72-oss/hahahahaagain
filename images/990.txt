This relaxed streetwear outfit features a stark, solid color blocking dominated by a bright white top and deep dark brown bottom. The upper layer is a heavyweight cotton crewneck t-shirt with an intentionally oversized silhouette, characterized by prominent dropped shoulders and wide, structural half-sleeves that extend to the elbows. The shirt drapes loosely over the hips, overlapping a pair of voluminous, wide-leg trousers made from a thick, matte cotton twill or canvas fabric. These loose-fitting pants feature subtle pleating or gathering at the waist and pool slightly over the footwear. The forearms and neck are the only exposed areas. On the feet are low-top, all-white leather skate or athletic sneakers with thick white rubber soles.
