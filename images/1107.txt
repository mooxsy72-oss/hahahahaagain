This monochromatic, edgy streetwear look features a deep, saturated black color scheme accented by subtle luminous glitters and metallic details. The dominant piece is a mid-thigh length, structured blazer dress made of heavy, shape-retentive deep black woven fabric infused with subtle, sparkling glitters. It features padded shoulders, long straight sleeves, a single-button closure, and standard notch lapels. Dramatic, symmetrical crescent cut-outs at the waist expose the midriff and flanks, with the upper edges lined in shimmering silver rhinestone fringe trim that hangs downward. Completing the look are calf-high deep black leather boots featuring a towering, ultra-high chunky platform sole and thick block heel, giving the lower silhouette a heavy, deliberate grounding.
