The outfit features an oversized, highly textured winter streetwear silhouette dominated by cream and beige tones, with a secondary brown monogram pattern and stark white footwear. A bulky, boxy puffer jacket provides the primary volume, constructed from thick, high-pile cream sherpa fleece on the body and sleeves, contrasting with a smooth, light khaki nylon material on the yoke and high stand-up collar. Layered underneath is a relaxed-fit, mid-weight cream knit v-neck sweater that drapes softly to the hips, featuring a ribbed collar and hem. Full-coverage, high-waisted tailored trousers in a stiff, structured tan fabric feature a dense, medium-scale brown interlocking geometric monogram pattern throughout; they maintain a crisp shape with prominent center creases and pool heavily around the feet. The footwear consists of prominent, bright white leather platform loafers with chunky, deeply lugged, oversized soles that anchor the voluminous proportions of the upper body.
