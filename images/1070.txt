This classic preppy academic ensemble features a rich autumnal palette dominated by burnt rust-orange, mustard ochre, dark forest-green tartan, and deep chocolate brown, accented by crimson. The dominant outer layer is a structured, hip-length tailored blazer in heavy matte burnt-rust wool suiting. It has notched lapels, a two-button single-breasted front with brown horn buttons, slightly padded shoulders, fitted full-length sleeves with three small cuff buttons, front flap pockets, and a left breast patch pocket adorned with an intricate, silver-toned embroidered heraldic crest topped by a crown. Worn unbuttoned over a fitted, high-crewneck ribbed-knit sweater in warm mustard ochre, the knit’s ribbed cuffs peek slightly beneath the blazer sleeves while its hem tucks neatly into the waist. A high-waisted, mid-thigh pleated mini skirt in medium-weight woven wool displays a dense, small-scale dark green, navy, and black tartan plaid crossed by thin yellow-ochre windowpane lines, flaring into crisp box pleats just below the blazer hem. Semi-opaque dark espresso-brown tights cover the legs down to knee-high, structured riding boots in glossy dark chocolate leather featuring round toes, low heels, and horizontal buckle straps around the ankles and top cuffs. A lightweight crimson silk neckerchief patterned with pink tonal swirls knots neatly around the neck, leaving only the hands and upper neck bare.
