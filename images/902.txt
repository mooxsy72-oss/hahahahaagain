OUTFIT: This modern casual menswear look relies on clean lines and a soft, refined palette of navy and cream. The dominant garment is a fitted navy knit polo shirt in fine textured cotton with a short open collar, a three-button placket, and short sleeves ending mid-bicep, neatly tucked in. It pairs with high-waisted cream wide-leg trousers with front pleats, a relaxed straight drape, and a slight break over the shoes. A slim brown leather belt defines the waist. Footwear consists of dark brown suede penny loafers. The only accessory is a minimalist silver watch with a black leather strap. COLOR PALETTE: navy, cream, dark brown, silver.
