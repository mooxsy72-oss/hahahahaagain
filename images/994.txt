This minimalist urban outfit features a relaxed, oversized silhouette with an earth-toned color distribution dominated by a solid olive-green top and secondary cream-white shorts. The upper layer is a heavyweight cotton crewneck t-shirt with an intentionally slouchy fit, characterized by distinct dropped shoulders and wide half-sleeves that extend to the elbows. The shirt drapes loosely past the hips, overlapping a pair of voluminous, wide-leg cream shorts. These relaxed-fit shorts are made from a mid-weight woven material that retains a structured drape and falls down to the lower knees. The forearms, neck, and lower legs are exposed. Mid-calf white ribbed sports socks lead into clean, low-top all-white leather sneakers with flat rubber soles. A silver metallic link-strap watch on the left wrist completes the look.
