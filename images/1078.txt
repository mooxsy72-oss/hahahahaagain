This ensemble features a rugged palette dominated by deep crimson red, dark indigo denim, and distressed dark brown leather, accented with matte black. The central outer garment is a cropped, waist-length asymmetrical biker jacket in heavy, semi-gloss crimson leather with structured shoulders, wide notched lapels, silver snap-button shoulder epaulets, an off-center silver front zipper, zippered slant pockets, a small snap-flap pocket, and zippered cuffs pushed up to mid-forearm. Centered on the upper back is a prominent embroidered patch featuring a winged orange-brown emblem framed by curved white banners reading "MADE IN" above and "HEAVEN" below. Worn open over a fitted, scoop-neck matte black jersey tank top tucked into the waist, the neckline leaves the upper chest and collarbones exposed. Form-fitting, ankle-length dark indigo blue skinny jeans in medium-weight faded stretch denim feature classic five-pocket construction, gold topstitching, and subtle thigh whiskering. A thick dark brown leather belt with a silver square buckle threads through the waistband, supporting an olive-drab canvas utility pouch on the left hip and a molded dark brown leather drop-leg holster secured around the right upper thigh by two horizontal buckled straps. Mid-calf, distressed dark brown leather lace-up combat boots with thick lugged soles and fingerless black leather biker gloves with knuckle cutouts and wrist straps complete the outfit.
