This contemporary evening gown features a monochromatic metallic silver-champagne palette dominated by dense, shimmering crystal mesh. The sleeveless, floor-length column maxi dress clings tightly to the figure before falling straight down from the hips to skim the ankles. It is constructed with wide, embellished shoulder straps, a low square front neckline, and a deep U-shaped open back that leaves the shoulders, upper chest, arms, and entire back exposed. Crafted from lightweight, semi-sheer silver mesh fabric completely encrusted in a tight, uniform grid of sparkling clear and silver-toned rhinestones, the material exhibits a fluid, heavy metallic drape with subtle transparency. Minimalist taupe pointed-toe stiletto sandals with single toe straps, delicate silver earrings, and a thin bracelet complete the look.
