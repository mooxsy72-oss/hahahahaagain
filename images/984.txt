This minimalist smart-casual outfit relies on a strict monochrome palette split equally between solid white and solid black. The top is a heavy, structured white cotton crewneck t-shirt with an oversized silhouette, featuring exaggerated dropped shoulders and wide sleeves that drape down to the elbows. It is cleanly tucked into high-waisted, tailored black trousers made from a mid-weight woven fabric with strong shape retention. The trousers feature deep double pleats at the waist, a sharp center crease along the legs, and a cropped, tapered fit that exposes the ankles. The forearms and lower ankles are the only exposed skin. On the feet are clean, low-top all-white leather sneakers with thick white rubber cup soles. A long black cord necklace with a flat, circular black disc pendant hangs down the chest, complemented by a minimalist black watch with a large round face on the left wrist.
