This retro-inspired, monochromatic summer streetwear outfit features a vibrant color distribution dominated entirely by uniform bright yellow tones, with no visual patterns.
The ensemble consists of an ultra-cropped yellow denim jacket in structured, heavyweight cotton, featuring a pointed collar, long button-cuffed sleeves, and buttoned flap chest pockets, left open. Underneath is a form-fitting yellow bustier crop top with molded cups, sweetheart neckline, thin shoulder straps, and a small central buckle, exposing the collarbones, chest, and a wide midriff section. This pairs with a matching high-waisted yellow mini skirt in a structured, clean-lined A-line silhouette, exposing the mid-thighs. Tight-fitting, opaque yellow thigh-high stockings cover the legs.
On the feet are yellow leather platform sandals with thick block heels. A thick, padded yellow headband crowns the head.
