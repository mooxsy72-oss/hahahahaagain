OUTFIT: This warm autumn menswear look centers on a heavy hand-knit sweater and classic tailored trousers. The dominant garment is a chunky charcoal-grey cable-knit sweater in thick wool with a high mock neck, raglan sleeves, deep braided cable panels down the front, and a regular fit, loosely tucked at the waist. It pairs with high-waisted chocolate-brown wool flannel trousers with double front pleats in a regular straight fit, neither slim nor wide, with a soft crease. A wide dark brown leather belt with a brass buckle defines the waist. Footwear consists of black leather Chelsea boots. COLOR PALETTE: charcoal grey, chocolate brown, black, brass.
