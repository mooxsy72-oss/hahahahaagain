The outfit features an oversized beige Nike sweatshirt layered over a white button-up shirt, paired with a charcoal pleated mini skirt, white crew socks, and black leather loafers. The thick, structured cotton-fleece beige sweatshirt dominates the upper body with intentionally voluminous, slouchy proportions, dropped shoulders, and tight ribbing at the crew neckline, cuffs, and hem. Beneath it, the crisp white cotton shirt peeks out at the pointed collar, the elongated cuffs, and the curved lower hemline, providing structural layering. The high-waisted charcoal-gray mini skirt is made from a heavy woven fabric with structured box pleats that flare outward, exposing the legs entirely. Polished black leather penny loafers with thick, chunky soles anchor the bottom of the look, paired with ribbed white cotton crew socks. A minimal black leather crescent shoulder bag with silver hardware completes the style.
