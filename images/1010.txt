The outfit features a cozy, layered winter aesthetic composed of an insulated bomber jacket, a cable-knit turtleneck sweater, relaxed trousers, and rugged boots. The dominant colors are earthy olive green and warm beige, with secondary cream tones and rich tan accents. Underneath, a thick, heavyweight cream sweater showcases a voluminous turtleneck collar and a distinct, dense vertical cable-knit pattern across the torso. The trousers are a relaxed, straight-leg fit in a solid beige cotton twill fabric, draping cleanly with minimal pooling over the footwear. Sturdy tan leather work boots anchor the look, featuring dark brown laces threaded through metallic speed hooks and a thick, lugged black rubber sole.
