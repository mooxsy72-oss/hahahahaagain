The winter streetwear outfit relies on a dark, texturally rich palette dominated by matte black and dark charcoal, accented by a sliver of white at the waist. The upper body's structure is defined by a heavy, thick black shearling aviator jacket featuring an oversized collar lined with dense, curly black wool fleece. Underneath, a dark knit top is layered over a longer, relaxed-fit white t-shirt that extends past the jacket's hem, creating a clean linear contrast. The lower half features relaxed, loose-fitting charcoal gray denim jeans that offer a soft drape, tapering slightly as they approach the ankle. The footwear consists of solid, chunky black leather Chelsea boots with a thick, structured sole that balances the heavy volume of the shearling jacket above.
