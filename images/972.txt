This elegant, modern evening ensemble features a uniform color distribution rendered entirely in a solid pastel ice-blue tone, creating a fluid and continuous monochrome silhouette.
The primary piece is a sophisticated two-piece dress set crafted from a lightweight, lustrous silk-satin fabric that exhibits a fluid drape and high-shine surface behavior. The upper section features a high, elegant boat neckline with crisp, asymmetrical structural pleats crossing over the bodice to create a cinched, wrapped waist effect. The long sleeves are constructed with a loose, relaxed fit that tapers into tight, elongated buttoned cuffs at the wrists. This overlaps smoothly with a matching high-waisted, floor-length maxi skirt that cuts closely through the hips before flowing down into a subtle, relaxed bias-cut flare.
Footwear consists of matching ice-blue satin dress shoes featuring a clean pointed toe line and a high stiletto heel.
