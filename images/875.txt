This fantasy-inspired resort outfit features a monochrome palette of warm, shimmering champagne-beige with no visual patterns, focusing entirely on texture and fluid drape.
The upper garment is an off-the-shoulder cropped peasant blouse crafted from a lightweight, lustrous satin fabric. It features a ruffled, elasticized neckline, puffed short sleeves with gathered elastic cuffs, and a smocked elastic hemline that cinches beneath the bust, leaving the shoulders, collarbones, and midriff fully exposed. A decorative thin cord laces through the center front, tying into a small bow.
The lower garment is a high-waisted, floor-length wrap maxi skirt made of semi-sheer, fluid chiffon that drapes softly to the ankles. It sits snugly on the waist and features a dramatic, overlapping high slit on the right side, completely exposing the right leg and thigh. On the feet are matching champagne-gold metallic sandals with thin, strappy laces that wrap tightly around the ankles and lower calves, set on slim stiletto heels.
