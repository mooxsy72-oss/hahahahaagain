OUTFIT: This mini dress is entirely deep ruby red, densely covered with small red sequins and glass beads that create a heavy, glittering surface. It has thin red rouleau straps and a straight neckline draped in soft gathered folds. The fitted corset-shaped bodice has curved vertical seams that define the waist. Large clusters of red teardrop crystals sit at both strap bases and on both hips. Below the hips, the beaded fabric is gathered at the sides into cascading drapes, adding volume and forming an uneven pointed handkerchief hem at mid-thigh, edged in an open beaded net texture. Footwear consists of dark red satin pointed-toe stiletto pumps. Accessories include small gold stud earrings. COLOR PALETTE: ruby red, dark red, gold.
