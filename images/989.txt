This minimalist summer streetwear outfit features a soft, neutral color palette dominated by an off-white top and secondary light beige shorts. The top is a heavyweight cotton crewneck t-shirt with an oversized, structured silhouette, featuring exaggerated dropped shoulders and wide sleeves that reach just above the elbows. It hangs loosely over the waistband of relaxed-fit, mid-thigh length beige chino shorts made from a lightweight canvas or linen-cotton blend. The shorts feature a clean elastic waistband with an adjustable matching beige drawstring. The lower legs, forearms, and neck are fully exposed. On the feet are low-top, all-white leather sneakers with a clean, low-profile design and white rubber soles, worn without visible socks. A thin, delicate silver chain necklace sits close to the collarbones, and a silver metallic link-strap watch wraps the left wrist.
