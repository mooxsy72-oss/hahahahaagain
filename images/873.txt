This casual Y2K-inspired streetwear outfit features a relaxed color distribution dominated by light grey and washed blue denim tones, excluding any bags, cameras, jewelry, or hand-held items.
The primary layer is an exaggeratedly oversized, light grey jersey t-shirt with a loose crew neckline and wide, elbow-length sleeves. It features a faded blue graphic print across the chest. Over this is a pair of slouchy, loose-fit light-blue denim overalls with one shoulder strap left unfastened and draped downward. The trousers feature a high-waisted fit secured by a plain black leather belt, a relaxed straight-leg silhouette, and wide cuffed hems at the ankles.
On the feet are classic low-top dark grey canvas sneakers with white rubber toe caps. A whimsical white plush beanie with long, floppy rabbit ears crowns the look.
