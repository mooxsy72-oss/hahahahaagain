This contemporary streetwear ensemble contrasts cropped military outerwear with dramatically oversized denim in a palette of faded sage-olive, woodland camouflage, and matte black. A heavyweight cotton-twill utility jacket in olive, brown, tan, and black camouflage is cropped abruptly below the chest with a raw, frayed hem, structured high stand collar, buttoned chest flap pockets, and oversized, elongated wide sleeves with buttoned cuffs. It is worn open over a tight, sleeveless black ribbed-knit crop top, leaving the upper chest and entire midriff bare. Dominating the lower silhouette are low-rise, baggy wide-leg jeans in heavy, washed sage-green denim with diagonal front seams, pooling over beige square-toe sandals. A black long-sleeve sweatshirt with white Gothic lettering along the sleeves knots around the hips.
