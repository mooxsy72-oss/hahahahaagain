This resort-inspired beachwear ensemble features a strict monochrome color distribution dominated entirely by a solid dark chocolate-brown tone, focusing on transparency and lightweight layering.
The innermost layer is a minimalist cropped bralette top featuring ultra-thin halter and back straps, crafted from a lightweight, translucent veil fabric that frames the collarbones and shoulders. Layered over it is an unbuttoned, oversized button-up shirt made from ultra-lightweight, fluid silk organza. It features a classic pointed collar, dropped shoulders, and loose billowy sleeves gathered into structured buttoned cuffs, pooling slightly around the wrists.
The lower garment consists of high-waisted, relaxed-fit beach shorts tailored from the same translucent chocolate-brown veil fabric. They feature an elasticized waistband with a matching adjustable drawstring bow and loose hems that end at the mid-thigh, highlighting the lower legs. On the feet are matching dark brown leather platform sandals featuring wide straps with double metallic buckles and thick, chunky rubber lug soles.
