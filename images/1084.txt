This contemporary Y2K streetwear ensemble contrasts minimal upper-body coverage with dramatically oversized denim in a palette of faded acid-wash blue-gray denim and matte black. A tight, strapless matte black stretch-jersey bandeau crop top covers only the bust, leaving the shoulders, arms, upper chest, and entire midriff bare. Dominating the silhouette are low-rise, baggy wide-leg cargo jeans constructed from heavy, stiff acid-wash blue-gray denim with distressed shredding along the knees. Multiple oversized, bulky three-dimensional bellows flap pockets stack along the thighs and calves, accented with dangling denim drawstring ties and cinched, pooling hems that cover pointed-toe black heels. Minimalist accessories include a delicate gold pendant necklace, layered silver bracelets, and a thin black wrist cord.
