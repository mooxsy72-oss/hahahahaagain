This casual, 90s-grunge-inspired outfit features a dominant solid black color palette contrasted by a secondary brown plaid pattern and bright white footwear. The central piece is a tight-fitting, mid-thigh length black slip dress crafted from soft, flexible ribbed knit fabric, featuring ultra-thin spaghetti straps and a low scoop neckline that leaves the collarbones, shoulders, and arms exposed. It layers beneath a highly oversized, loose-fitting brown and beige plaid button-up shirt with long sleeves, draped open to introduce relaxed upper-body volume.
Footwear consists of exaggeratedly bulky, oversized white platform sneakers with a thick, sculpted sole, paired with thick, slouched white ribbed socks that sit just above the ankle. Wearable accessories include a layered silver chain necklace, silver hoop earrings, a square-faced silver link watch, rectangular dark-tinted sunglasses, a black croc-embossed leather top-handle bag, and a black satin hair scrunchie worn on the wrist.
