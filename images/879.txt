This resort-inspired contemporary summer outfit features a clean neutral color palette dominated by solid off-white and beige tones, balanced by dark brown leather and straw textures.
The inner layer is a tight, ribbed knit crop top in light beige with a deep scoop neckline, leaving the midriff exposed. Over it rests an oversized, unbuttoned long-sleeved shirt in fluid, lightweight off-white linen that drapes loosely past the hips. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, structured off-white cotton shorts with tailored front pleats and cuffed hems ending at the upper thigh, exposing the legs.
On the feet are flat dark-brown leather slide sandals with a distinctive cutout strap design. Accessories include a large rectangular woven straw tote bag with brown leather shoulder straps, slim gold-framed oval sunglasses, a delicate gold pendant necklace, and small gold hoop earrings.
