OUTFIT: This cozy minimalist winter look contrasts a soft light knit dress with chunky black boots. The dominant garment is a light greige ribbed knit midi dress with a high crew neckline, long full-length sleeves slightly flared at the cuffs, and a close body-hugging fit falling to mid-calf, defined by wide vertical ribs. Footwear consists of black glossy leather Chelsea boots with elastic side panels, pull tabs, and a chunky lug platform sole. Accessories include a gold square-face watch with a black dial and gold mesh strap, worn on top of the knit sleeve at the wrist. COLOR PALETTE: greige, black, gold.
