The layered, monochromatic streetwear outfit consists of loose-fitting knit and fleece garments in varying gray tones, creating a relaxed, soft-draping silhouette. The upper half is built on three distinct layers: a voluminous mid-weight heather gray half-zip hoodie with its hood pulled up, a dark gray textured sweater peeking out at the collar, and a longer light gray base t-shirt extending straight past the hem. Full-coverage, relaxed-fit charcoal gray sweatpants in a soft, thick fleece sit comfortably at the waist and drape loosely down the legs, bunching slightly at the ankles.
