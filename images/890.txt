This casual, vibrant streetwear outfit features a playful color palette dominated by bright orange tones, balanced by a secondary lavender accent and a bold houndstooth pattern, excluding any handbags or necklaces.
The core layers consist of a form-fitting orange crop top with a V-neckline, exposing the collarbones and a sliver of the midriff. Over this is an oversized, boxy cardigan made of medium-weight knit fabric. It features a round collarless neckline, a front button placket, and voluminous bishop sleeves with long lavender ribbed cuffs. The body is decorated with a medium-scale orange and lavender houndstooth pattern. This pairs with a high-waisted orange denim mini skirt in a structured A-line silhouette with a central seam and front button closure, exposing the thighs.
On the feet are orange patent leather platform sandals with thick block heels and ankle straps. A matching solid orange canvas bucket hat and small gold star-shaped stud earrings complete the look.
