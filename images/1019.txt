The casual winter outfit utilizes an earthy, warm color palette combining brown tones with cream and white, emphasizing soft, cold-weather textures. The primary visual volume comes from an oversized, puffy down jacket in a matte golden-brown shade, featuring deep square quilting, an integrated heavily padded hood, and drawstring details at the hem. Underneath, a thick heather-brown textured knit sweater provides secondary bulk, designed with long sleeves and a high, folded turtleneck collar. The lower body features straight-leg, slightly cropped corduroy trousers in a soft cream color, showing a vertical ribbed texture and a structured drape that stops just above the ankles. The look is completed with white crew socks and clean white leather low-top sneakers with contrasting brown rubber gum soles.
