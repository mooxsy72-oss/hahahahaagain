OUTFIT: This modern autumn street look pairs an olive leather trench with a sleek black knit dress. The dominant garment is an oversized olive-khaki faux-leather trench coat falling below the knee, with wide notch lapels, epaulettes, a double-breasted front with dark buttons, buckled cuff straps, and a self-tie belt at the waist, worn open. Beneath it is a black ribbed knit sleeveless mini dress with a high mock neck and a close body-hugging fit ending at the upper thigh. Footwear consists of olive suede ultra-short sheepskin boots on a chunky platform sole, worn with slouchy white knit socks bunched above them. Accessories include chunky gold teardrop earrings and thin gold bangles. COLOR PALETTE: olive khaki, black, white, gold.
