This modern gothic-inspired dramatic outfit features a stark contrast color distribution dominated by a solid white top and solid black footwear, with no visual patterns.
The primary garment is an oversized, thigh-length shirt dress crafted from crisp, structured white cotton poplin. It features a wide, plunging unbuttoned collar that drops off the shoulders, exposing the neckline and collarbones. The silhouette is defined by exaggerated, voluminous bishop sleeves gathered at dropped shoulder seams and loosely cinched into wide cuffs. The natural waist is aggressively cinched by a wide corset belt made of stiff, structured black leather, featuring a curved hourglass shape and a functional front lace-up closure with thin black cords through metal eyelets.
On the feet are dramatic, tight-fitting thigh-high boots made of smooth black leather. They feature pointed toes, high stiletto heels, and long leather fringe tassels draping from the outer upper thighs. Wearable accessories include a delicate, layered silver chain necklace with a small circular hoop pendant resting on the chest.
