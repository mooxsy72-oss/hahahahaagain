A contemporary avant-garde floor-length evening gown in matte black crepe with sparkling silver rhinestone accents. The right side features a sharp, exaggerated padded shoulder and a fitted, full-length sleeve, while the entire left shoulder, arm, chest, midriff, and upper hip remain completely bare of black fabric. Pleated black fabric drops straight down from the right shoulder, sweeping diagonally across the lower torso into a low-slung, voluminous draped cowl on the left hip, leaving a wide, open diagonal cutout across the front. Inside this cutout sits a narrow, strapless silver-encrusted bandeau across the bust and two parallel crystal straps spanning the bare left hip. A sleek black floor-length column skirt with a trailing right side panel and black pointed-toe heels complete the look.
