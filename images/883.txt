This casual summer streetwear outfit features a vibrant, youthful color palette combining a lavender-purple outer layer with classic blue denim and solid white accents, completely excluding any shoulder bag or necklace.
The upper layer is a cropped, boxy lavender denim jacket made from structured, heavyweight cotton denim. It features a pointed collar, buttoned flap chest pockets, long sleeves with buttoned cuffs, and a raw, unfinished cropped hemline that sits above the waist. Underneath is a form-fitting white cropped t-shirt with a crew neckline, exposing a small section of the midriff. The lower body is defined by a high-waisted, short blue denim mini skirt with a subtly distressed, raw-edged hemline, exposing the legs.
On the feet are low-top white leather sneakers with a prominent lavender Nike swoosh logo, worn over pulled-up lavender crew socks.
