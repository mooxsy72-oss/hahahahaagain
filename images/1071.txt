This ensemble belongs to a casual indie streetwear and modern teen-grunge tradition, defined by heavy layered textures. Muted olive-khaki, charcoal black, and faded blue denim dominate the palette, accented by matte black. The outer layer is a structured, heavyweight olive-khaki cotton parka jacket featuring a textured, crinkled matte weave, snap-placket front, and a high storm collar. It is worn open over a thick, heavyweight charcoal-black fleece hoodie with a prominent, structured hood. Fitted, faded blue skinny jeans with a distressed, ripped right knee and raw, frayed ankle hems form the lower half. A plain black baseball cap and worn gray-green high-top canvas sneakers with rubber toe caps complete the look.
