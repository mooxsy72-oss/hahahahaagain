OUTFIT: This warm-toned autumn menswear look centers on a rust knit and dark flannel tailoring. The dominant garment is a chunky rust-brown cable-knit mock-neck sweater in thick wool with a ribbed high collar, braided cable panels down the front, raglan sleeves, and a regular fit, loosely tucked at the waist. It pairs with high-waisted charcoal-grey wool flannel trousers with double front pleats in a regular straight fit, neither slim nor wide. A dark brown leather belt with a brass buckle defines the waist. Footwear consists of dark brown suede Chelsea boots. COLOR PALETTE: rust brown, charcoal grey, dark brown, brass.
