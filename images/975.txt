This modern evening ensemble features a monochrome color distribution dominated by a solid dark burgundy-red dress, accented by metallic silver footwear and jewelry.
The centerpiece is a floor-length, column-silhouette evening gown crafted from a mid-weight, structure-retaining matte crepe fabric that transitions into a fluid drape. It features a unique cutout neckline constructed with thin spaghetti straps, a horizontal fabric band above the bust, and a wide oval chest cutout. The garment includes a tightly fitted, elongated corset bodice with a curved basque waistline that layers over a wrapped tulip-style skirt. The skirt features asymmetric gathering on the right hip, creating a high, dramatic thigh-length side slit that completely exposes the right leg.
Footwear consists of metallic silver leather sandals featuring a minimalist open-toe strap layout, a slender ankle strap, and a high stiletto heel. Accessories are completed by long, sculptural silver linear drop earrings.
