OUTFIT: This sophisticated autumn look relies on a monochrome palette of soft grey and silver accents. The dominant garment is a long, flowing grey wool cape coat with slits for the arms, a clean collarless neckline, and a fluid silhouette falling to mid-calf. Beneath it is a fitted pale grey turtleneck knit tucked into a high-waisted pleated midi skirt in silvery grey satin with a gentle shimmer. Footwear consists of grey suede pointed pumps with a slender stiletto heel and a delicate ankle strap. Accessories include silver statement earrings, a thin silver belt at the waist, and a small structured grey leather handbag. COLOR PALETTE: dove grey, silver, pale grey, charcoal.
