This contemporary casual streetwear ensemble features a dark monochrome palette dominated by slate charcoal gray and washed vintage black denim, accented with crisp white and metallic silver. An oversized, boxy waist-length t-shirt in heavyweight matte charcoal-gray cotton jersey features a thick ribbed crew collar, dropped shoulders, and wide, elbow-length short sleeves, leaving the neck and forearms bare. Subtle tonal arched "YOUNGLA" embroidery sits across the left chest. Dominating the lower silhouette are baggy, wide-leg jeans in heavy, structured washed black-gray denim with vertical fade creases and long hems pooling over black-and-white leather low-top court sneakers. Minimalist accessories include a thin silver chain necklace with a small rectangular gold pendant and a metal link wristwatch.
