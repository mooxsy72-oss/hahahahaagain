This modern autumn outfit features a classic color distribution dominated by deep solid black, contrasted by a voluminous beige outer layer and a touch of gold hardware.
The primary silhouette-defining garment is a loose, ankle-length trench coat made of thick, structure-retaining beige cotton-gabardine, featuring an oversized double-breasted layout, dramatic wide notched lapels, long relaxed sleeves with buttoned cuff straps, and a matching fabric belt tightly cinched at the waist. Layered underneath is a tight, form-fitting black jersey turtleneck top with long slim sleeves providing complete upper-body coverage. This tucks into high-waisted, flared black faux-leather shorts with distinct front pleats that create an A-line shape.
Footwear consists of matching over-the-knee black leather boots with a slightly slouched, gathered shaft texture and a chunky lugged sole profile.
