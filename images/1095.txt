This contemporary tailoring ensemble features a dominant grey glen plaid pattern balanced by a secondary solid black tone, with subtle beige accents within the checkered grid.
The outermost layer is a structured, hip-length blazer crafted from a medium-weight, stiff wool blend that retains sharp shoulders and a straight silhouette. It features classic notched lapels, a single-button waist closure, and long, slim sleeves, leaving the neck and collarbones exposed. Underneath, a tight, cropped bandeau top made of matching grey plaid fabric covers the bust while exposing a broad section of the midriff.
The lower garment is a high-waisted mini-skirt tailored from the same grey plaid wool, featuring a straight A-line cut that ends at the upper thigh. On the feet are dramatic, tight-fitting black leather over-the-knee boots with chunky block heels and thick platform soles, which heavily dominate the lower silhouette and leave only a narrow strip of skin visible on the thighs. Large, thick silver hoop earrings finish the look.
