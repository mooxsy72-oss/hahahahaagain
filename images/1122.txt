This contemporary athleisure outfit features a high-contrast black and off-white color palette, balanced between a bulky upper layer and form-fitting base garments. Layered open over the torso is a relaxed, hip-length off-white fleece full-zip hoodie with dropped shoulders, long sleeves, ribbed cuffs and hem, kangaroo pockets, and a hood with drawstrings. Underneath, a tight black stretch-knit scoop-neck sports bra with a white central swoosh logo leaves the collarbone, upper chest, and midsection bare. High-waisted, second-skin black compression leggings extend to the lower calves, featuring a wide waistband and a small white swoosh on the left thigh. Completing the look are white ribbed crew socks with black swooshes and all-white mesh running sneakers.
