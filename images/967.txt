This tailored modern casual outfit features a stark color distribution dominated by a solid jet-black jacket and a light-blue denim base, grounded by dramatic black footwear.
The primary piece is a structured, fitted jacket made of thick, shape-retaining jet-black suiting fabric. It is constructed with a collarless deep V-neckline, a central vertical row of fabric-covered buttons, an integrated wide cinched waistband, and a flared peplum hem detailed with crisp, structured box pleats. The long sleeves feature subtle puff shoulder gathers that taper smoothly down to tight wrists. This pairs with high-waisted jeans crafted from a dense, flexible light-wash blue stretch-denim that hugs the hips and thighs tightly before dramatically flaring outward from the knee down to the floor, creating a distinctive slim-flare silhouette.
Footwear consists of statement black leather pumps featuring an ultra-high stiletto heel and a thick, towering front platform sole.
