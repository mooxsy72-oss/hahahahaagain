This casual summer street outfit features a relaxed color distribution dominated by off-white and light-wash blue denim, accented by vibrant royal-blue graphics and warm gold hardware.
The primary silhouette-defining piece is a loose, oversized short-sleeved t-shirt made of mid-weight, comfortable off-white cotton jersey. It features a classic ribbed crew neckline and wide half-length sleeves. The front is decorated with a large, centered royal-blue graphic print showing a cartoon coffee cup character, the stylized text "ESPRESSO yourself," and a vertical checkered banner pattern. The t-shirt is loosely tucked into high-waisted denim shorts crafted from structured, light-wash blue cotton denim, featuring aggressively frayed, raw-cut hems that expose the thighs.
Footwear consists of white leather low-top sneakers with a thick rubber sole, detailed with a prominent royal-blue swoosh logo on the side, worn with white ribbed crew socks featuring two thin royal-blue horizontal stripes around the cuffs. Accessories are completed by a layered gold chain necklace with a small pendant, stacked gold chain link bracelets on both wrists, a gold watch, and multiple thin gold rings.
