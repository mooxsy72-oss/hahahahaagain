OUTFIT: This romantic modern mini dress is made entirely of ivory jacquard with a soft pearly shimmer and a subtle tonal floral texture. The bodice is fitted and structured with vertical princess seams, shaping the waist. The bust has sculpted cups with scalloped wavy upper edges and a small keyhole cutout at the center. Thin straps are made of strung pearls and small sparkling crystal rosettes, exposing the shoulders, collarbones, and arms. From the waist flares a short, very full circle skirt in stiff jacquard that holds a wide bell-shaped volume with deep soft folds, ending at mid-thigh. Footwear consists of ivory satin pointed-toe stiletto pumps. Accessories include small pearl drop earrings. COLOR PALETTE: ivory, pearl white, silver.
