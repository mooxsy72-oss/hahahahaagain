This relaxed streetwear outfit features a loose, oversized silhouette with a color distribution of dominant washed charcoal grey on top and secondary light heather grey on the bottom. The top is a heavy cotton crewneck t-shirt with exaggerated dropped shoulders, wide sleeves cuffed at the mid-arm, and a long hem falling below the hips. The chest features a large graphic print with red script text reading "Vacation Days!" above a blue and white coastal sailing scene. This is paired with exceptionally wide, voluminous light heather grey jersey sweat shorts that extend past the knees, creating an intentionally baggy profile. Mid-calf white ribbed sports socks lead into chunky, retro-style athletic sneakers featuring a complex mesh and metallic silver overlay pattern with thick white rubber soles.
