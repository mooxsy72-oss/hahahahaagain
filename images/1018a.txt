The winter streetwear outfit relies on a muted taupe and matte black palette, focusing entirely on an oversized, un-accessorized silhouette. The upper body structure is defined by an oversized, boxy blouson jacket made from a thick, heavy-weight boiled wool or textured fleece in a speckled taupe color, featuring dropped shoulders, full sleeves, a relaxed classic collar, and an open silver zipper closure. Underneath, a matching tonal taupe sweater is worn, cleanly covering the torso. The lower body contrasts in loose, relaxed-fit black sweatpants made from dense fabric that drapes heavily and cinches in folds around the ankles. The footwear consists of gray tech-running sneakers with layered mesh and synthetic paneling.
