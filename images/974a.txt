This contemporary outfit features a sleek, form-fitting silhouette. The dominant layer is a skin-tight, full-length black jumpsuit or catsuit made from a matte, highly elastic fabric that hugs the body from the chest down to the ankles. It features a low, curved sweetheart or scoop neckline that exposes the collarbones and upper chest, with long slim sleeves and thin decorative shoulder straps.
On the feet are heavy, chunky black leather combat boots with thick platform soles and front zipper details.
A delicate metallic chain necklace with a small pendant rests on the exposed upper chest, completing the high-contrast silhouette.
