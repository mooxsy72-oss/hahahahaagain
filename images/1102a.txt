This monochrome, modern streetwear outfit features an all-black dominant color palette with metallic gold highlights. The key layering piece is a mid-thigh length black trench-style jacket made from structured, matte woven fabric, featuring oversized notch lapels, wide long sleeves with buckled cuffs, and a self-tie waist belt creating structural volume. It layers over a tight-fitting, lightweight black long-sleeved top with a crew neck, providing full upper-body coverage. The top pairs with high-waisted, full-length black ribbed leggings that offer a sleek, close-to-skin silhouette. Footwear consists of knee-high black leather boots with a thick, flat rubber tread sole. Accessories include a thin gold chain necklace, a gold wristwatch, and a slim gold bracelet.
