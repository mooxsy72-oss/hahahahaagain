The outfit presents a minimalist, smart-casual summer aesthetic composed of a tank top, pleated trousers, a belt, sneakers, a crossbody bag, and a wristwatch. The dominant colors are taupe and solid white, accented by deep brown leather details. The clean white, ribbed cotton tank top features a tight, form-fitting silhouette with a scooped neck and narrow shoulder straps, exposing the shoulders, collarbones, and arms. The high-waisted taupe trousers are tailored from a lightweight, smooth woven fabric, showcasing dual front pleats, sharp ironed creases, and a tapered silhouette that finishes above the ankle with a neat cuff. A smooth, medium-brown leather belt with a silver-toned square buckle secures the waist. Accessories include a small, rectangular brown leather crossbody pouch with a thin shoulder strap, a metallic silver watch with a circular face, and low-top white leather sneakers with matching white laces.
