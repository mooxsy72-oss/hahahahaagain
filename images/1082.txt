This contemporary streetwear ensemble contrasts a cropped sporty top with dramatically oversized denim, featuring a palette of burgundy, crisp white, muted olive-gray denim, and woodland camouflage. A medium-weight cotton jersey rugby polo shirt is cropped abruptly beneath the bust, leaving the entire midriff exposed. It features a crisp white polo collar, open placket, and wide horizontal burgundy and white stripes across the loose bodice and slouchy, full-length sleeves with white ribbed cuffs. Dominating the lower silhouette are low-rise, baggy wide-leg jeans in heavy, washed olive-gray denim with classic five-pocket construction, pooling over pointed-toe burgundy heels. Accessories include a long gold lariat necklace and a medium woodland-camouflage bowler handbag with tan leather handles and a dangling ornate gold cross charm.
