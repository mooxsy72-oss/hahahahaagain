OUTFIT: This luxe modern winter look pairs dramatic black fur with soft grey knitwear. The dominant garment is an oversized long black faux-fur coat in a maxi length reaching the lower calf, almost to the ankles, with long glossy shaggy pile, a huge wide notched collar, dropped shoulders, and voluminous straight sleeves, worn open. Beneath it is a charcoal-grey chunky ribbed knit half-zip sweater with a wide folded spread collar, a silver metal zipper, dropped shoulders, and a relaxed boxy fit ending at the hips. It pairs with high-waisted black leggings with a wide flat waistband. Footwear consists of charcoal-grey suede ultra-short sheepskin boots on a thick platform sole. Accessories include small silver hoop earrings. COLOR PALETTE: black, charcoal grey, silver.
