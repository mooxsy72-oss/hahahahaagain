This romantic summer streetwear outfit features a fresh, feminine color palette dominated by a patterned sage green skirt, balanced by a solid white top and coordinated green footwear, completely excluding any necklace or handheld bag.
The core ensemble consists of a form-fitting white cropped t-shirt with a high crew neckline and short sleeves, exposing the midriff. It is paired with a high-waisted, midi-length sage green skirt crafted from lightweight, fluidly draping woven fabric. The skirt features a dense, small-scale floral print of pink rosebuds and is constructed with a dramatic vertical ruched drawstring seam down the left side, creating an adjustable high leg slit. Thin white fabric ties secure the waistband in a delicate front bow.
On the feet are sage green strappy leather sandals with high stiletto heels and intersecting laces wrapping up the ankles. Wearable accessories include chunky gold hoop earrings and stacked gold bracelets on the left wrist.
