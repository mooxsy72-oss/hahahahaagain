This cohesive, monochrome autumn outfit is rendered entirely in a solid dark chocolate-brown palette, accented by warm gold hardware.
The primary piece is a fitted, high-coverage jersey top with structured, padded power shoulders, a clean mock neckline, and tight full-length sleeves. This tucks into matching high-waisted tailored shorts made of a thick, structure-retaining wool-blend fabric with crisp front pleats, creating a structured A-line silhouette. The waist is cinched by a thin matching leather belt with a sleek gold rectangular buckle, providing a clear division between the pieces.
Footwear consists of pointed-toe ankle boots in smooth, matte leather, featuring a very high, thin stiletto heel. Wearable accessories are completed by chunky gold teardrop earrings and a gold dress watch with a circular white dial.
