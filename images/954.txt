OUTFIT: This luxurious winter look uses soft grey and white. The dominant garment is a light grey wool maxi coat reaching the ankles, with a straight relaxed silhouette and full-length sleeves, worn open. Its most striking feature is a huge, voluminous fox-fur shawl collar in white with dark grey streaks, wrapping the neck and running down both front edges past the waist. Beneath it is a fitted white long-sleeve top with a wide boat neckline, tucked into a white high-waisted pencil skirt. Footwear consists of light grey suede pointed-toe stiletto ankle boots. Accessories include a silver serpent-shaped watch coiling around the wrist over the sleeve, with a diamond-set oval face. COLOR PALETTE: light grey, white, dark grey, silver.
