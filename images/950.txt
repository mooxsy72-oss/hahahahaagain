OUTFIT: This elegant modern winter look pairs a sleek knit dress with a dramatic black fur coat. The dominant garment is a long black faux-fur coat falling below the knee, with dense glossy pile, wide notch lapels, a relaxed straight silhouette, and long straight sleeves, worn open. Beneath it is a mocha-brown ribbed knit midi dress with a long pointed shirt collar, a full button-front placket with small tonal buttons, long fitted sleeves, and curved vertical seams creating a close body-hugging fit, with a front slit opening from the lower buttons. Footwear consists of black stretch sock ankle boots with a pointed toe and a high block heel. Accessories include delicate gold drop earrings. COLOR PALETTE: black, mocha brown, gold.
