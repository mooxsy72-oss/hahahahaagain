This chic, feminine outfit features a dominant solid black palette balanced by a secondary cream-white layer with a bold polka-dot pattern and gold jewelry accents. The upper piece is a lightweight, cropped cream-white sleeveless top adorned with a dense, small-scale black polka-dot pattern. Its construction features thin shoulder straps tied in bows, a high straight neckline, and a gathered ruffle peplum hem that flares at the waist, exposing the arms and collarbones. It pairs with high-waisted, full-length black wide-leg trousers made from structured, mid-weight suiting fabric that drapes smoothly down to the feet. Footwear consists of matte black Mary Jane flats with an almond toe and a gold buckle strap. Accessories include a small black knotted shoulder bag, chunky gold hoop earrings, and a rectangular gold link watch.
