This contemporary casual streetwear outfit features a solid pastel pink and black color palette, heavily dominated by voluminous, oversized sweatpants. A tight, fitted black cotton jersey short-sleeve crew-neck t-shirt covers the torso and shoulders, leaving the arms bare from mid-bicep down. Tucked cleanly into the top is a pair of heavy, thick pastel pink fleece sweatpants with an exaggerated, baggy barrel-leg silhouette that balloons outward before gathering at elasticated ankle cuffs. The high-rise multi-stitched elastic waistband features an external drawstring with metal aglets, side seam pockets, front vertical pintuck seams, and faint tonal embroidery on the left thigh. Completing the look are low-top pastel pink suede and leather sneakers with matching laces, white side stripes, and dark soles.
