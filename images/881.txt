This contemporary casual streetwear outfit features a balanced color distribution dominated by a solid light-wash denim-blue tone, paired with secondary crisp white garments and minimalist silver accents.
The upper garment is a tight, form-fitting cropped tank top in solid white, made from a smooth, elastic cotton jersey knit that retains its shape. It features a scoop neckline and sleeveless construction with wide shoulder straps, leaving the arms, collarbones, and midriff exposed. The lower garment consists of high-waisted, straight-leg mom-style jeans crafted from thick, structured light-wash blue denim. They feature a classic five-pocket design, a zippered fly with a top button, and straight hems resting at the ankles.
On the feet are low-top white leather retro sneakers with tonal laces and thick rubber soles. Wearable accessories include a delicate, thin silver chain necklace.
