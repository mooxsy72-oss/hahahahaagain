This casual, modern outfit features a light color palette dominated by dusty periwinkle blue and crisp white, with accents of medium-wash denim, brown leather, and clean white footwear.
The primary layer is a loose, slightly oversized long-sleeved button-up shirt crafted from sheer, lightweight dusty-blue floral lace, featuring a standard pointed collar, white front buttons, and a small rectangular black text patch on the single chest pocket. Underneath layers a tight, cropped white jersey camisole with thin shoulder straps, leaving the collarbones, midriff, and arms partially exposed through the sheer lace fabric. The top layers are paired with high-waisted, relaxed-fit wide-leg jeans in structured medium-wash blue denim, secured at the waist by a thin brown leather belt with a simple metallic buckle.
Completing the look are clean white low-top leather sneakers featuring a thick, flat platform sole and classic tonal laking.
