This modern, collegiate-inspired casual outfit features a solid neutral palette dominated by jet black and light heather grey, accented by deep burgundy leather and polished gold hardware details.
The primary garment is a cropped, slim-fit button-up cardigan blouse made of semi-sheer black floral lace fabric. It features a simple crew neckline, a central row of small polished gold buttons, and long semi-sheer sleeves that flare dramatically into wide bell cuffs at the wrists, leaving the midriff exposed. This pairs with a low-waisted, ultra-short mini skirt in thick, structure-retaining light-grey wool cloth, tailored with sharp, structured knife pleats that flare into an A-line shape. The waist is defined by a thin burgundy leather belt featuring a small gold buckle.
Footwear consists of sleek black leather heeled sandals featuring a minimalist open-toe strap layout, a delicate ankle wrap closure, and a thin stiletto high heel.
