OUTFIT: This ensemble belongs to a tactical field-agent and survival-action tradition, pairing casual tailoring with functional combat gear. Off-white, muted olive-gray, and matte black dominate the palette, accented by dusty steel-blue. Distressed gray-brown lace-up heeled ankle boots complete the look.
