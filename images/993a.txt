This earth-toned minimalist streetwear outfit features a balanced color distribution dominated by a solid chocolate-brown top and secondary light cream-beige trousers. The upper layer is a heavy, boxy cotton crewneck t-shirt with an oversized silhouette, characterized by distinct dropped shoulders and wide half-sleeves that drape to the elbows. It hangs loosely past the waist, overlapping a pair of voluminous, wide-leg cream trousers. Made from a mid-weight woven material with a fluid, soft drape, these fluid pants feature deep vertical pleats at the high waist and puddle slightly over the footwear. The forearms and neck are the only exposed skin.
On the feet are chunky grey athletic running sneakers with layered white paneling and black mesh inserts.
Accessories include a thin silver chain necklace with a circular pendant and a silver link-strap watch on the left wrist.
