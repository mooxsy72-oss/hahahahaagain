The feminine ensemble focuses on a structured pastel pink tweed suit paired with a deeply contrasting maroon lace inner layer, completely excluding bags. The outer layer is a cropped, structured light pink tweed jacket featuring a round neckline, long straight sleeves with decorative button cuffs, and a buttonless front worn open. This coordinates with a high-waisted pink tweed mini skirt that has a straight silhouette and a distinctive scalloped bottom hem. Underneath, a form-fitting, semi-sheer maroon lace corset top provides bold texture, constructed with defined underwire cups and exposed vertical boning. Footwear consists of light pink stiletto sandals heavily embellished with dimensional tulle fabric rosettes along the toe and ankle straps. Wearable accessories are limited to a delicate gold chain necklace and gold floral statement earrings.
