OUTFIT: This minimalist monochrome menswear look focuses on soft knit textures in shades of grey. The dominant garment is a heather-grey quarter-zip sweater in medium-weight merino wool with a high stand collar, a silver metal zipper, ribbed cuffs, and a relaxed fit sitting at the hips. It pairs with charcoal wool-blend tapered trousers with a flat front and a clean crease running down each leg. Footwear consists of black leather derby shoes with a rounded toe and slim rubber sole. Accessories include a simple black leather belt and a minimalist steel watch. COLOR PALETTE: heather grey, charcoal, black, steel.
