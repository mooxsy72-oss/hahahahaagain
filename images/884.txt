This romantic summer streetwear outfit features a vibrant color palette dominated by purple and lavender tones, accented with neutral brown footwear, excluding any shoulder bag.
The upper layer consists of a sheer, form-fitting lavender mesh top with a high crew neckline and long sleeves. It is adorned with an all-over repeating pattern of small purple hearts. Underneath is a matching opaque lavender strapless bandeau top, covering the chest while leaving the upper shoulders and a sliver of the midriff exposed through the mesh. The lower body is defined by high-waisted, structured lavender tailored shorts featuring front pressed pleats, a buttoned waistband closure, and an A-line silhouette exposing the thighs.
On the feet are brown leather gladiator sandals with flat soles and thin straps wrapping around the ankles.
