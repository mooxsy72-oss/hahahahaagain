OUTFIT: This refined autumn menswear look pairs rugged leather with rich wool textures. The dominant garment is a black leather flight jacket in soft, slightly worn grain leather with a wide shirt collar, a front zip, two large flap patch pockets with snap buttons, ribbed knit cuffs and hem, and a regular fit ending at the waist, worn open. Beneath it is a cream donegal wool turtleneck with a thick rolled collar and fine brown and grey flecks. It pairs with dark brown herringbone wool trousers with a single front pleat in a regular straight fit, neither slim nor wide. Footwear consists of dark brown suede chukka boots. COLOR PALETTE: black, cream, dark brown.
