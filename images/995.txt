This casual streetwear outfit relies on a cozy, high-volume silhouette with a color distribution dominated by a solid charcoal-grey top layer and secondary cream-white elements. The primary upper layer is an exceptionally oversized, heavyweight fleece hoodie in charcoal grey, featuring deeply dropped shoulders, baggy sleeves bunched at the ribbed cuffs, a crossover hood neckline, and a spacious front kangaroo pocket. Peeking from beneath its ribbed hem is a thin layer of a white cotton undershirt. This pairs with relaxed-fit, knee-length sweat shorts in a thick cream fleece fabric with an elastic waistband. Hands, wrists, neck, and lower legs are exposed. On the feet are off-white canvas high-top sneakers with white rubber toe caps and laces, worn over mid-calf ribbed cream sports socks. A textured black nylon fanny pack is worn as a crossbody bag across the chest, paired with a thin silver chain necklace.
