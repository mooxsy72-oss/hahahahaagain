OUTFIT: This elegant autumn menswear look relies on fine ribbed knitwear and soft tailoring. The dominant garment is a long-sleeve knit polo in muted slate blue with a wide vertical rib, a spread collar, an open V-placket without buttons, and a close regular fit, neatly tucked into the waist. It pairs with high-waisted stone-taupe wool trousers with double front pleats and a sharp crease, in a regular straight fit, neither slim nor wide. A slim dark brown leather belt defines the waist. Footwear consists of dark brown leather penny loafers. The only accessory is a steel watch with a metal bracelet. COLOR PALETTE: slate blue, taupe, dark brown, steel.
