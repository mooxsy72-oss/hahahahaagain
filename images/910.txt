OUTFIT: This clean minimalist streetwear look is built on simple solid basics in grey and black. The dominant garment is a heather-grey heavyweight cotton t-shirt with a thick ribbed crew neckline, dropped shoulders, and a slightly boxy fit with short sleeves ending just above the elbows, worn untucked. It pairs with black relaxed-fit cotton twill trousers with a straight wide leg and a clean full-length hem. Footwear consists of minimalist white leather low-top sneakers. The only accessory is a thin black leather strap watch. COLOR PALETTE: heather grey, black, white.
