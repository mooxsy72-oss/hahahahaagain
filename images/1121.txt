This monochromatic casual streetwear outfit features a solid heather grey palette anchored by crisp white footwear, heavily dominated by voluminous oversized sweatpants. A form-fitting, lightweight heather grey stretch-jersey short-sleeve crew-neck t-shirt covers the torso and shoulders, leaving the arms bare below the mid-bicep and featuring horizontal ruching gathered across the waist. Below, heavy, thick heather grey cotton-fleece sweatpants create an exaggerated, baggy barrel-leg silhouette that balloons outward before gathering at elasticated ankle cuffs. The high-rise gathered elastic waistband features side seam pockets and a tiny white embroidered swoosh logo on the left thigh. Completing the look are chunky, low-top all-white smooth leather sneakers with perforated toe boxes, white laces with metallic lace locks, and thick rubber soles.
