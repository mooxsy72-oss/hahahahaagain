This contemporary streetwear ensemble features a neutral palette of warm beige, off-white, and taupe, dominated by dramatically oversized trousers paired with an asymmetrical cropped sweater. A medium-weight off-white knit pullover has an off-the-shoulder boat neckline exposing the right shoulder, while its raw-edged hem cuts sharply upward from the right hip to under the left bust, leaving the midriff bare. Voluminous, slouchy long sleeves taper into ribbed cuffs. Low-rise, wide-leg taupe trousers in smooth, fluid suiting fabric feature front pleats, white side piping, a small thigh logo, and pooling floor-length hems over taupe suede sneakers with white toe logos. Accessories include a fitted taupe knit beanie with white graphic detailing, gold cross pendant necklace, hoop earrings, and rings.
