The outfit features a cropped dark-gray wool jacket worn over a form-fitting black bodysuit, paired with a matching dark-gray pleated mini skirt, black leather loafers, and a cream shoulder bag. The heavy, structured dark-gray jacket dominates the upper silhouette with intentionally boxy, oversized proportions, a classic point collar, large tonal front buttons, and wide, loose sleeves that end near the wrists. Beneath it, a matte black bodysuit with a low scoop neckline fits tightly over the torso, exposing the collarbones and neck. The high-waisted, dark-gray mini skirt is crafted from a thick woven wool-like fabric with crisp, structured box pleats that flare sharply outward, exposing the legs entirely. Polished black leather penny loafers with low block heels anchor the bottom of the look. A contrasting cream-colored leather shoulder bag with a gold chain strap serves as a secondary visual element, complemented by small, chunky gold hoop earrings.
