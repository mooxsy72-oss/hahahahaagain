OUTFIT: This minimalist winter look contrasts a light knit dress with heavy black fur. The dominant garment is an oversized black faux-fur coat in a long maxi length reaching the ankles, longer than the dress, with dense plush velvety pile, wide notch lapels, dropped shoulders, and wide full-length sleeves extending straight down past the wrists, worn open. Beneath it is a light greige ribbed knit midi dress with a high crew neckline, long sleeves slightly flared at the cuffs, and a close body-hugging fit falling to mid-calf. Footwear consists of black glossy leather Chelsea boots with elastic side panels and a chunky lug platform sole. COLOR PALETTE: black, greige.
