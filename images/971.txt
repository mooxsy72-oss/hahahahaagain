This modern romantic outfit features a coordinated color distribution of creamy white fabric decorated with a small-scale, sparse print of muted blue and green florals, grounded by stark white footwear.
The focal garment is a structured mini dress made of a mid-weight, crisp cotton cloth that retains its fluid form. It features a plunging deep V-neckline framed by an integrated tall neck-choker collar with long, dramatic fabric scarf extensions draping down the front. The silhouette is defined by exaggerated, padded power shoulders and long tapered sleeves, balanced by a fitted waist that flares into a voluminous, tiered ruffled A-line skirt ending at mid-thigh.
Footwear consists of statement white leather pumps featuring an ultra-high stiletto heel and a thick, towering front platform sole, while large white pearl button earrings complete the accessories.
