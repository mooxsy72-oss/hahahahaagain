This avant-garde, fantasy-inspired evening gown features a monochromatic pink palette designed with an organic, sculptural silhouette resembling a blooming flower.
The primary garment is an asymmetrical, strapless petal-shaped cocktail dress constructed from stiff, highly structured translucent polymer or iridescent glass-like organza. The material has high shape retention and a brilliant, glistening sheen embedded with fine glitter flakes. The bodice is composed of overlapping, curved panels forming a plunging sweetheart neckline. The dress features a dramatically cinched natural waistline before flaring into a voluminous, structured multi-layered skirt composed of large, overlapping pointed petals that cover the hips and thighs.
On the feet are matching crystalline pink stiletto pumps crafted from clear tinted PVC with towering heels.
