This contemporary outfit features a stark contrast in volume and color, combining a form-fitting black base with an oversized camel-brown jacket. The dominant base layer is a skin-tight, full-length black jumpsuit or catsuit made from a matte, highly elastic fabric that hugs the body from the chest down to the ankles. It features a low, curved sweetheart or scoop neckline that exposes the collarbones and upper chest, with thin shoulder straps. Layered over this is a short, voluminous aviator-style jacket in a warm camel-brown suede-like material. The jacket is cropped short above the waist and has intentionally oversized, puffy sleeves and wide notch lapels lined with thick, textured white sherpa fleece, creating dramatic upper-body volume. On the feet are heavy, chunky black leather combat boots with thick platform soles and front zipper details. A delicate metallic chain necklace with a small pendant rests on the exposed upper chest, completing the top-heavy, high-contrast silhouette.
