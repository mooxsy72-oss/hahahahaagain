This modern streetwear outfit features a bold, high-contrast color palette dominated by sleek solid black and stark white, accented by deep burgundy text and silver hardware.
The primary layer consists of a form-fitting, skin-tight t-shirt crafted from a lightweight, elastic white cotton jersey. It is constructed with a mid-height mock neckline and short sleeves, featuring the word "HONEY" printed across the chest in large, bold burgundy serif block letters. The t-shirt is tucked into high-waisted, form-fitting black stretch-denim skinny jeans, cinched at the waist by a wide black leather belt with a polished square silver buckle.
Footwear consists of statement black leather pumps featuring an ultra-high stiletto heel and a thick, towering front platform sole, while a chunky gold snake-shaped wrap ring completes the accessories.
