OUTFIT: This chic autumn ensemble centers on a fluid satin skirt paired with a crisp, feminine blouse. The dominant garment is a bias-cut midi slip skirt in liquid chocolate-brown satin that skims the hips and flows softly around the calves with a subtle sheen. On top is a soft white silk blouse with a deep V-neckline and long sleeves with buttoned cuffs, neatly tucked into the high waist of the skirt. Draped over the shoulders is a cropped tan suede jacket with a short pointed collar. Footwear consists of chocolate suede knee-high boots with a slim stiletto heel and an almond toe. Accessories include gold hoop earrings and a fine layered chain necklace. COLOR PALETTE: chocolate, white, tan, gold.
