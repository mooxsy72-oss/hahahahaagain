The outfit features a minimalist, monochromatic streetwear aesthetic composed of a puffer jacket, a long-sleeve top, pleated wide-leg trousers, and sneakers. The dominant color is deep matte black, which covers nearly the entire silhouette, creating a unified block of color contrasted only by solid crisp white footwear at the base. The top layer is an unusually bulky, down-padded black puffer jacket with a high, structured stand-up collar, prominent horizontal quilting, and a zippered front left open. Beneath the jacket is a relaxed-fit, plain black long-sleeve crewneck top made from smooth, lightweight cotton jersey. The high-waisted black trousers are exceptionally wide-legged and fluid, featuring neat dual front pleats that expand into a voluminous drape, pooling slightly over the clean white leather low-top platform sneakers.
