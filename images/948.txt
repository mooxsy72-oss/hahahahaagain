OUTFIT: This cozy modern autumn look pairs sleek black basics with soft cream textures. The dominant garment is a cropped, boxy cream faux-shearling jacket with a smooth suede-like surface, wide notch lapels lined with plush cream sherpa, dropped shoulders, and sherpa trim on the cuffs, hem, and patch pockets, ending at the waist and worn open. Beneath it is a fitted black long-sleeve stretch jersey top with a wide scoop neckline. It pairs with high-waisted black leggings with a wide flat waistband. Chunky cream cable-knit leg warmers are slouched around the lower legs above taupe suede ultra-short sheepskin boots on a thick platform sole. Accessories include small gold hoop earrings. COLOR PALETTE: cream, black, taupe, gold.
