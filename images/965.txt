This tailored modern casual outfit features a solid neutral color palette dominated by deep espresso brown and stark white, accented by deep burgundy footwear.
The primary piece is a structured, fitted jacket made of thick, shape-retaining espresso-brown suiting fabric. It features a deep V-neckline without a collar, a central row of fabric-covered buttons, a cinched waistband, and a flared peplum waist constructed with crisp, vertical box pleats. The long sleeves are designed with subtle puff shoulder gathers that taper down to tight wrists. This pairs with high-waisted white trousers crafted from a dense, flexible stretch-cotton fabric that hugs the hips and thighs tightly before dramatically flaring outward from the knee down to the floor, creating a distinctive slim-flare silhouette.
Footwear consists of dark burgundy leather dress shoes featuring a sharp pointed toe line and a high stiletto heel.
