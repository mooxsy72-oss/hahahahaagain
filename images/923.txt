OUTFIT: This rich autumn menswear look pairs a suede jacket with soft cream knitwear. The dominant garment is a chocolate-brown suede bomber jacket with a ribbed knit collar, cuffs, and hem, a front zip, and a regular fit ending at the waist, worn open. Beneath it is a cream ribbed knit crewneck sweater tucked into the waist. It pairs with light grey wool flannel trousers with double front pleats in a regular straight fit, neither slim nor wide. Footwear consists of dark brown suede loafers. The only accessory is a steel watch with a brown leather strap. COLOR PALETTE: chocolate brown, cream, light grey.
