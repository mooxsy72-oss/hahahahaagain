This athleisure ensemble relies on a high-contrast monochrome palette dominated by black, with stark white secondary pieces. The foundation consists of a tight, skin-fitting black elastic crop top with a straight bandeau neckline and thin shoulder straps, paired with matching high-waisted, full-length black leggings that hug the lower body entirely. This tight-fitting base exposes the midriff and arms. Adding volume around the upper chest and shoulders is a heavy, thick white cotton-blend sweatshirt draped casually capelike with its sleeves knotted loosely at the front center. Bright white, vertically ribbed crew socks are pulled taut over the lower leggings. Footwear features chunky white athletic sneakers with technical black and grey structural overlays on the sides. Accessories include a structured off-white six-panel baseball cap with a contrasting black embroidered logo on the front, thick-framed dark sunglasses, a slim white electronic wristwatch, and a thin gold bangle.
