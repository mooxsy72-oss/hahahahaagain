This modern, winter casual outfit features neutral solid tones of charcoal grey, heather grey, white, and beige.
The dominant piece is an oversized, cropped puffer jacket in textured charcoal-grey cable knit with horizontal quilting, featuring full-volume sleeves and a chunky high stand collar with a front zipper closure. Layered underneath is a secondary body-con white jersey top, presenting a simple crew neckline, fitted waist construction, and slim full-length sleeves providing complete upper-body coverage.
Complementing the top are high-waisted, wide-leg tailored trousers in thick, structure-retaining heather-grey wool fabric with front pleated details draping down to the ankle.
Accessories consist of oversized, plush faux-fur earmuffs in a warm light-beige tone and sleek white faux-leather ankle boots featuring a sharp pointed toe line and a thin stiletto high heel.
