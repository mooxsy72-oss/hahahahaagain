This ensemble belongs to a minimalist urban techwear and monochrome tactical streetwear tradition, contrasting a sleek upper-body silhouette with voluminous lower-body proportions. Matte jet-black dominates the palette, accented by a single silver pendant and black hardware. The primary layer combines a form-fitting, ribbed black turtleneck with thumbhole cuffs tucked into high-waisted, baggy black cargo pants. The pants feature front waist pleats, a belt-loop D-ring, oversized side-thigh flap pockets, and drawstring-cinched ankle cuffs. A delicate silver necklace with an ornate pendant rests over the chest, while chunky all-black lace-up tactical sneakers anchor the look.
