This high-fashion evening ensemble features a dominant solid black color distribution accented by a prominent, glittering gold secondary tone, displaying no graphic patterns.
The main garment is a tea-length strapless dress crafted from heavy, structured black satin that retains rich, deep pleats and a voluminous flare. It has a fitted, boned bodice with a straight neckline, fully exposing the shoulders and collarbones, and cinches tightly at the natural waist. A dramatic, deep vertical slit on the left side extends to the upper thigh, exposing the left leg.
Accessories dominate the silhouette. A wide, oversized black wool-felt sun hat sits horizontally, draped with a dense, dramatic fringe of cascading gold bead strands that veil the face and neck. On the feet are black leather stiletto sandals featuring intricate, multi-strap gold chain laces wrapping up the ankle. The outfit is completed by a small, structured black leather cylindrical barrel bag.
